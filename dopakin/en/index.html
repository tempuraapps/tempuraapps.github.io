Dopakin

Locked until the time you set.

Lock social and short-video apps for exactly as long as you decide. A lock can’t be ended from inside the app, so “just a quick look” is harder to start. When time is up, everything opens again on its own.

iPhone only (iOS 18.0 or later)

In an emergency, turn off Screen Time for Dopakin in iOS Settings and the lock is removed right away. Dopakin is a self-control tool for your own iPhone.

Less to set up. A lock that stays on.

  • Choose what to lock

    Pick individual apps, whole categories, or websites (up to 50 apps and 50 websites). Choosing a category locks every app in it.

  • Set it like a timer

    Turn a wheel to set the length, from 5 minutes up to 24 hours in 5-minute steps. Or pick the time it should end instead. (Locks longer than 2 hours need Dopakin Pro.)

  • No early exit inside the app

    During a lock, the only change you can make is to extend it; nothing in the app shortens or stops it. Apps unlock automatically when time is up. In an emergency, turning off Screen Time for Dopakin in iOS Settings removes the lock right away.

  • History to look at, images to share

    See your locked time for today, the last 7 days, and all time. There are no streaks, goals, or rankings. You can share a pledge, a finished lock, or your last 7 days as an image.

Three steps

  1. 1

    Set the time

    Set a length on the wheel, or pick an end time.

  2. 2

    Choose apps

    Pick the apps or categories you want locked. Your selection is kept for next time, and you can remove or reorder items in the list on the Lock tab.

  3. 3

    Start

    Tap “Start” on the confirmation screen and the lock begins. Apps unlock when time is up.

The first time, you’ll be asked to allow Screen Time access. Dopakin can’t be used on some iPhones, such as one with no passcode set or one where Screen Time is managed by a parent or guardian.

Questions

Can I end a lock early?

Not from inside Dopakin. In an emergency, turn off Screen Time for Dopakin in iOS Settings and the lock is removed right away.

Will it lock the apps I need to stay in touch?

Only the apps, categories, and websites you choose are locked. Choosing a whole category locks every app in it, so to keep the apps you use to stay in touch open, choose apps one by one instead.

What if an app is still locked after time’s up?

First, tap “Unlock” or “OK” on the locked app’s screen. If it’s still locked, open Dopakin. The full steps are on the support page.

How much can I do without paying?

Locks of up to 2 hours, as often as you like. A monthly summary in History, extra designs for share images, Theme Colors (other than Coral), and longer locks (up to 24 hours) come with Dopakin Pro, a one-time purchase.

Is the list of apps I chose sent anywhere?

No. Your app selection and your lock history are stored only on your device. To improve the app we measure anonymous usage, such as a lock being started and the length that was set. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Can I use it to manage a child’s iPhone?

No. Dopakin is for your own iPhone only. It can’t be used on an iPhone where Screen Time is managed by a parent or guardian.

What you lock stays between you and your iPhone

Your app selection and your lock history are stored only on your device. No account is needed, and nothing is gathered on our servers. To improve the app we measure anonymous usage (a lock being started or finished, the length that was set), but never which apps you chose. The Privacy Policy has the details.
