Dopakin Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

About the app · Support · 日本語

Dopakin (“the App”) is provided by TempuraApps (“we”). This policy explains how the App handles user information.

1. Information we collect

The App does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. Which apps, categories, and websites you chose to lock is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to or stored on our servers or those of any third party. The list that makes up your lock history is also kept on your device, and the list itself is not sent anywhere. However, as part of the usage analytics described in section 4, the App sends an external service anonymous records that a lock was started or finished, along with details such as the length that was set. For the purchase processing described in section 5, it also sends an external service information such as your purchase status. Neither includes your name, nor which apps, categories, or websites you chose.

2. Use of Screen Time

To keep the apps, categories, and websites you choose closed for the length of time you set, the App uses Apple’s Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls, Managed Settings, and Device Activity). This requires you to allow Screen Time access yourself. The App uses only the authorization meant for managing your own device; it has no features for a parent to manage a child’s device.

What you choose is stored on your device as opaque tokens issued by iOS. The tokens can’t be read as app names, so we have no way of knowing which apps you chose. That information is never sent off the device and is never used for analytics, advertising, or disclosure to third parties.

The App does not read device usage such as how long each app is used or what you have viewed.

You can withdraw this access at any time in iOS Settings. Withdrawing it removes any lock in progress.

3. Accounts and network access

No account is required to use the App. Which apps you chose stays on your device and is not sent anywhere. The App does, however, communicate with a small number of external services for the usage analytics described below (section 4, which includes anonymous records of locks being started and finished) and for purchase processing (section 5).

4. Usage analytics (PostHog)

To improve quality and features, the App measures anonymous usage with the analytics service PostHog. What is collected is the type of action taken, listed below, together with the numbers and categories that accompany it. Each record carries the date and time of the action.

  • Launching or updating the App
  • Finishing the introduction shown on first launch
  • Whether Screen Time access was allowed, and which screen the request was made from
  • Saving your apps to lock (only two facts: whether a category is included, and whether any always-allowed app is set — never which apps or categories)
  • Starting a lock, finishing a lock, and a lock ending early (the length that was set in minutes, whether Pro is active, and a category for why it ended early)
  • A lock failing to start, and a category for the reason
  • Opening the paid plan screen and where it was opened from; starting a purchase, a purchase succeeding or failing (the plan type and a category for the reason); restoring a purchase

We do not send personal information such as your name or email address, nor which apps, categories, or websites you chose.

Technical information automatically attached by PostHog may also be included: the app version, the operating system type and version, the device type, and the app’s display language. Time zone is stripped before sending, because it can suggest an approximate region. IP addresses are configured to be discarded on the analytics side, and we have specified that no region or location be inferred from them. Finer locations such as your city or your coordinates are never collected. None of this is intended to identify a specific individual, and it is not linked to any account. Data is stored in the EU region.

Each record sent carries an anonymous identifier created for that installation of the App. It exists so that actions from the same installation can be grouped together. It is not tied to your name, email address, account, or any hardware identifier provided by the device, and we do not use it to identify you. Deleting and reinstalling the App produces a new one.

We have no way to connect that identifier to you as a person. For that reason we cannot act on requests to retrieve or delete one user’s data individually. If you wish to stop the measurement entirely, please delete the App; nothing is sent after deletion.

The App shows no advertising and performs no ad tracking (IDFA or otherwise). We do not use the collected information for advertising, and we do not provide or sell it to third parties.

5. Purchases (RevenueCat)

The paid feature (Dopakin Pro) is a one-time purchase. Purchases and restores are handled through Apple’s App Store, and we use the billing infrastructure service RevenueCat to manage purchase status. As a result, information such as your entitlement status is processed by RevenueCat. Payment details such as credit card numbers are handled by Apple and are never received by us. Purchases are managed under an anonymous identifier and are not linked to personal information such as your name or email address. RevenueCat is a service operated by a company based in the United States.

6. Information stored on your device

The App stores the following on your device. It is kept in an area of the device that only the App and its extensions can read and write, and it is not sent to our servers.

  • Your selection of apps to lock and your always-allowed apps (the tokens described in section 2)
  • The state of a lock in progress (start time, end time, and length)
  • Your lock history (start time, end time, length, and whether the lock finished or ended early)
  • A record of when each lock was removed and how (kept to check that the App is working; it contains no app names)
  • Settings such as the last length you chose and your appearance setting

If you have iOS backups turned on (iCloud Backup, for example), this information may be included in your own backup. Backups are provided by Apple for your account, and we cannot access them.

7. Sharing images

The App can turn a pledge, a finished lock, or your week into an image for sharing. The image is created on your device and goes only to the person or app you pick in the iOS share sheet. We do not receive it. The image does not contain the names or icons of the apps you locked.

8. Disclosure to third parties

We do not provide or sell your information to third parties for advertising purposes. The information sent to the external services described in sections 4 and 5 (PostHog and RevenueCat) is processed in accordance with each service’s own privacy policy.

9. Deleting your data

Data stored on your device can be erased with “Delete All Data” inside the App (it deletes your history and app selection), or by deleting the App itself. The analytics data described in section 4 is anonymous, so one user’s portion cannot be singled out and deleted.

10. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App cannot be used on a device where Screen Time is managed by a parent or guardian.

11. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy as features are added or changed. Significant changes will be announced on this page.
