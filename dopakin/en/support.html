Troubleshooting

These are the same steps as “Troubleshooting” in the app, found on the Settings tab and on the screen shown during a lock.

Still locked after time’s up

  1. Tap the button on the locked app
  2. If it’s still locked, open Dopakin

Opening a locked app shows a screen with the end time and “Dopakin is on.” Once the time has passed, tapping the button on that screen removes the lock. Opening Dopakin also removes any lock that is past its end time.

Unlock in an emergency

  1. Open iOS Settings
  2. Turn off Screen Time for Dopakin

A lock removed this way appears in History as “Ended early” and isn’t counted in your total. To use Dopakin again, open Dopakin in iOS Settings and turn on Screen Time.

Frequently asked questions

Can I end a lock early?

Not from inside Dopakin. In an emergency, turning off Screen Time for Dopakin in iOS Settings removes the lock right away.

Can I change the time during a lock?

You can only make it longer. On the screen shown during a lock, tap “Extend” and choose +15 min, +30 min, or +1 hr. Once extended, it can’t be shortened. The total after extending can be up to 2 hours without Pro, and up to 24 hours with Pro. A lock can’t be extended in its final minute.

How long can a lock be?

From 5 minutes to 24 hours. The wheel sets the length in 5-minute steps, or you can pick an end time instead. Up to 2 hours is available without Pro; anything longer needs Dopakin Pro.

How do I choose which apps to lock?

In the iOS picker, choose apps, categories, or websites. iOS doesn’t let Dopakin select apps for you. You can pick up to 50 apps and 50 websites; choosing a category locks any number of apps.

How do I remove an app or change the order?

In the “Apps to Lock” list on the Lock tab, swipe left on a row and tap “Remove.” Right after removing, you can tap “Undo” to bring it back. To change the order, tap “Reorder,” or touch and hold a row and drag it. To add apps or choose again, tap “Choose Apps”; “Show All” opens the full list. You can’t make these changes during a lock.

I don’t want the apps I use to stay in touch to be locked

Choosing a category locks every app in it. To keep the apps you use to stay in touch open, choose the apps you want to lock one by one instead of a category (up to 50).

What happens when I open a locked app?

You see a screen with the end time and “Dopakin is on.” When time is up, the lock is removed automatically and the app opens as usual. If it’s still locked, see Troubleshooting.

Why does Dopakin ask for Screen Time access?

Dopakin uses it to lock the apps you choose. Your app choices stay on your iPhone. Without it, a lock can’t be started. To allow it later, open Dopakin in iOS Settings and turn on Screen Time.

It says “Couldn’t get Screen Time access”

If you declined, open Dopakin in iOS Settings and turn on Screen Time. Access can’t be allowed on some iPhones, such as one with no passcode set or one where Screen Time is managed by a parent or guardian. If it says “Couldn’t connect,” check your connection and try again.

Can I use it to manage a child’s iPhone?

No. Dopakin is for your own iPhone only. It has no parental-control features.

What does History keep?

The date, time, and length of each lock. The History tab shows your total, today’s time, the number of finished locks, the last 7 days, and the full list. A lock that ended early is listed as “Ended early” and isn’t counted in the total. With Dopakin Pro, you also get a monthly summary. History doesn’t record which apps were locked.

What is in the image when I share?

The mascot, plus figures such as the length of the lock, its end time, or your total for the last 7 days. With Dopakin Pro, you can also pick extra designs in other colors. The names and icons of the apps you locked aren’t included. The image goes through the iOS share sheet only to the person or app you pick.

Is my data synced to my other devices?

No. Your history and your app selection are stored only on that iPhone.

How do I delete my history?

“Delete All Data” on the Settings tab deletes your history and app selection. This can’t be undone, and it isn’t available during a lock. Your Pro purchase isn’t affected.

What happens if I delete the app?

The history and app selection stored on the device are erased. If you bought Pro, reinstall and use “Restore Purchases” to get it back.

How do I turn on dark mode?

It’s available without Pro. Choose “Automatic,” “Light,” or “Dark” under “Appearance” on the Settings tab.

About Dopakin Pro

What do I get with Pro?

Three things:

  • Locks longer than 2 hours, up to 24 hours
  • A monthly summary in History
  • Extra designs for share images (two other color schemes)

The number of locks, app selection, automatic unlocking, history (today, the last 7 days, all time, and the full list), and sharing work the same without Pro.

How much is it?

It’s a one-time purchase. The price is shown on the purchase screen in the app and varies by country or region.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once and keep it. Nothing renews, and there’s nothing to cancel.

I purchased Pro but it isn’t showing up

Try “Restore Purchases” on the plan screen. You need to be signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you used to purchase. If it still doesn’t appear, contact us at the address above.

Will Pro carry over to a new iPhone?

Yes. Sign in with the same Apple Account on the new iPhone and use “Restore Purchases.” You may need to choose your apps to lock again on the new iPhone.

Do I have to pay to unlock?

No. When time is up, the lock comes off the same way with or without Pro. The troubleshooting steps above also work whether or not you’ve purchased anything.

I’d like a refund

Payments are handled by Apple, and Apple decides whether a refund can be given. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We can’t process refunds ourselves.
