Troubleshooting

These are the same steps as “Troubleshooting” in the app, found on the Settings tab and on the screen shown during a lock.

Still locked after time’s up

  1. Open the locked app and tap Unlock
  2. If it’s still locked, open Dopakin

Opening a locked app shows a screen with the end time and “Dopakin is on”. Once the time has passed, tapping Unlock on that screen removes the lock. Opening Dopakin also removes any lock that is past its end time.

Unlock in an emergency

Turning off Screen Time for Dopakin in iOS Settings removes the lock right away.

“Open Settings” under Troubleshooting in Dopakin takes you to Dopakin’s page in iOS Settings.

A lock removed this way appears in History as “Ended early” and is not counted in your total. To use Dopakin again, open Dopakin in iOS Settings and turn on Screen Time.

Frequently asked questions

Can I end a lock early?

Not from inside Dopakin. In an emergency, turning off Screen Time for Dopakin in iOS Settings removes the lock right away.

Can I change the time during a lock?

You can only make it longer. On the screen shown during a lock, tap Extend and choose +15 min, +30 min, or +1 hr. Once extended, it can’t be shortened. The total after extending can be up to 30 minutes without Pro, and up to 8 hours with Pro. A lock can’t be extended in its final minute.

How long can a lock be?

From 5 minutes to 8 hours. The wheel sets the length in 5-minute steps, or you can pick an end time instead. Up to 30 minutes is available without Pro; anything longer needs Pro.

How do I choose which apps to lock?

In the iOS picker, choose apps, categories, or websites. iOS does not let Dopakin select apps for you. You can pick up to 50 individual apps; choosing a category locks any number of apps. To change your selection later, go to “Apps to Lock” on the Settings tab (it can’t be changed during a lock).

I don’t want the apps I use to stay in touch to be locked

Add them to “Always Allowed” on the “Apps to Lock” screen. Even if you selected a whole category, the apps listed there stay open. You can add up to 50 apps there, chosen individually.

What happens when I open a locked app?

You see a screen with the end time and “Dopakin is on”. When time is up the lock is removed automatically and the app opens as usual.

Why does Dopakin ask for Screen Time access?

Dopakin uses it to lock the apps you choose. Your app choices stay on your iPhone. Without it, a lock can’t be started. To allow it later, open Dopakin in iOS Settings and turn on Screen Time.

It says “Screen Time isn’t available on this iPhone”

Dopakin can’t be used without a passcode, or when Screen Time is managed by a parent or guardian. Access can also fail when the device is offline, so check your connection and try again.

Can I use it to manage a child’s iPhone?

No. Dopakin is for your own iPhone only. It has no parental-control features.

What does History keep?

The date, time, and length of each lock. The History tab shows your total, today’s time, the number of finished locks, the last 7 days, and the full list. A lock that ended early is listed as “Ended early” and is not counted in the total. History does not record which apps were locked.

What is in the image when I share?

The mascot, plus figures such as the length of the lock, its end time, or your total for the week. The names and icons of the apps you locked are not included. The image goes through the iOS share sheet only to the person or app you pick.

Is my data synced to my other devices?

No. Your history and your app selection are stored only on that iPhone.

How do I delete my history?

“Delete All Data” on the Settings tab deletes your history and app selection. This can’t be undone, and it is not available during a lock. Your Pro purchase is not affected.

What happens if I delete the app?

The history and app selection stored on the device are erased. If you bought Pro, reinstall and use “Restore Purchases” to get it back.

How do I turn on dark mode?

It is available without Pro. Choose Automatic, Light, or Dark under Appearance on the Settings tab.

About Dopakin Pro

What does Pro unlock?

Locks longer than 30 minutes, up to 8 hours. That is the only difference. App selection, Always Allowed, automatic unlocking, history, and sharing work the same without Pro.

How much is it?

It is a one-time purchase. The price is shown on the purchase screen in the app and varies by country or region.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once and keep it. Nothing renews, and there is nothing to cancel.

I purchased Pro but it is not showing up

Try “Restore Purchases” on the plan screen. You need to be signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you used to purchase. If it still does not appear, please contact us at the address above.

Will Pro carry over to a new iPhone?

Yes. Sign in with the same Apple Account on the new iPhone and use “Restore Purchases”. You may need to choose your apps to lock again on the new iPhone.

Do I have to pay to unlock?

No. When time is up, the lock comes off the same way with or without Pro. The troubleshooting steps above also work whether or not you have purchased anything.

I would like a refund

Payments are handled by Apple, and Apple decides whether a refund can be given. Please apply at reportaproblem.apple.com. We are not able to process refunds ourselves.
