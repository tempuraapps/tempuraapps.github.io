FukuMeguri

Go visit the shops you love.

Group the shops that caught your eye by neighborhood, and see them all on one map. Every visit you log lands on the calendar, along with the shops you went to most that month. Your records stay on your iPhone. No account, no ads.

iOS 18.0 or later / iPhone

Six tools for a day of browsing

  • Group by neighborhood

    Harajuku, Shimokitazawa, Koenji. Create an area and add your shops to it. Seeing them grouped by neighborhood makes it easier to pick where to head on your next day off.

  • See them on the map

    Every shop you have saved shows up as a pin. Which street to start from, whether to walk one station further — you can settle the route before you leave the house.

  • Log the day you went

    Tap “Visited today” on a shop’s page. Visits stack up on the calendar, and the shops you went to most that month are listed for you.

  • A wishlist per shop

    Save the clothes that caught your eye with a photo and a note. Each one is tied to a brand and a shop, so “where did I see that again?” stops happening.

  • Search by brand or tag

    Find shops across every area by the brands they carry, or by tags you made yourself. Vintage, select, close to the station — how it is organized is up to you.

Export and import your records as JSON (importing and data-only export are free; exporting with photos is FukuMeguri Pro).

Your shop records stay on your device

Areas, shops, visits, wishlist items, brands, tags, photos and notes are stored only on your device. Nothing syncs to another device and nothing is sent to our servers. The exception is showing a map or searching for a place, where the visible area or your search terms go to Apple’s map services. See the Privacy Policy for details.
