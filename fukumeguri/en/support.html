Frequently asked questions

Where do I start?

On the Browse tab, create one area and add a shop to it. An area can be whatever unit you actually walk — a neighborhood, a street, a station. Each shop can carry a map pin, the brands it stocks, tags, photos and notes.

Can I try it before entering my own shops?

Yes. Choose "Try the sample town" on the last page of the intro, or go to Settings > Data > Add sample data. That fills the app with a sample set of areas and shops so you can see every screen. You can remove the sample data from the same place, and anything you entered yourself stays.

How do I record that I visited a shop?

Tap "Visited today" on the shop's page. The days you record appear on the calendar, and the shops you went to most that month are listed below it. A summary of the whole year is under "Year in Review" on the calendar tab. You can also add a past date afterwards, or delete a visit you logged by mistake.

Do I have to allow location access?

No. Areas, shops, visits, the calendar, Year in Review, your wishlist, brands and tags all work without it. Location is only needed to show where you are on the map, to place a shop from your current position, and to look up the nearest station. You can change the permission at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > FukuMeguri.

Does my data sync to my other devices?

It does not. Areas, shops, visits, wishlist items, brands, tags, photos and notes are stored only on your device. To move them to a new phone, use the export function under Settings > Data to save a JSON file, then import it on the new device. Export and import are FukuMeguri Pro features.

Is anything about my shops or photos sent elsewhere?

No. Shop details, visits and photos stay on your device. The exception is showing a map and searching for a shop location or the nearest station, where the visible area or your search terms go to Apple's map services. That does not pass through our servers, and we do not store it. See the Privacy Policy for details.

What happens to my data if I delete the app?

The areas, shops, visits, wishlist items, photos and notes stored on your device are removed with it. If you want to keep any of it, export a file from Settings > Data before deleting.

If I delete a shop, what happens to its visits, photos and wishlist items?

The visits and photos attached to that shop are deleted with it. Wishlist items are not deleted — only their link to that shop is removed, and the items stay on the Wishlist tab. Deleting an area also deletes the shops inside it. Before either, a confirmation shows you how much will be removed.

About FukuMeguri Pro

What does Pro unlock?

It unlocks exporting and importing your data as JSON. Photos can be included in the export, which makes it useful when changing phones or when you simply want a backup in hand.

Everything else — adding areas and shops, the map, visit records, the calendar, Year in Review, your wishlist, brands, tags, photos, and changing the theme and app icon — is free, with no limit on how much you can add. Pro exists to give you a way to back things up, and to support continued development.

I purchased Pro but it is not showing

Try "Restore purchases" in the app. You need to be signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you used to buy it. If it still does not appear, please contact us at the address above.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Cancellation happens on the App Store side. Open Settings > your name > Subscriptions, choose FukuMeguri, and select "Cancel Subscription". "Manage subscription" under Settings > Plan in the app opens the same screen.

After cancelling you keep Pro until the period you already paid for ends. Cancelling on its own does not issue a pro-rated refund; if you want a refund, see "I would like a refund" below.

If I cancel, what happens to the data I exported?

Files you already exported stay with you, and nothing on your device is deleted — your areas, shops, visits and photos all remain. What stops after cancelling is creating new exports and importing files.

How do I check my renewal date?

The same Settings > your name > Subscriptions screen shows your next renewal date and price. Renewal continues automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends.

I would like a refund

Payment is handled by Apple, and refund decisions are Apple's to make. Please apply at reportaproblem.apple.com. We are not able to process refunds ourselves.
