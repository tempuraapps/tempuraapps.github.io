One Good Deed

One good thing a day.

You gave up your seat. You picked up a piece of litter. Small things are exactly the ones you forget if you don't write them down. Thirty seconds a day, one entry at a time. No account, no ads.

iOS 18.0 or later / iPhone

Write it, keep it up, look back

  • Write today's good deed

    One a day, up to 100 characters, with hashtags if you want them. On days when nothing comes to mind, the app offers an idea — tap through until one fits and it becomes your entry.

  • Watch your calendar light up

    Every day you write, a seasonal mark lights up. A month's worth is visible at a glance, and you can open any past day to read it again.

  • Recap

    Your current streak, your longest streak, and your total number of entries, With Pro, add a By tag view of the month you're looking at and a list of your streak milestones.

  • Colours that follow the season

    Four palettes — spring, summer, autumn, and winter. With Pro, let them change automatically with the month, and take the home screen icon along with them if you like.

A single daily reminder, at whatever time you choose. Pro adds JSON export and import for your entries.

What you write stays on your iPhone

The text of your entries and your hashtags are stored only on your device and are never sent to a server. No account is required. The app does send anonymous usage statistics to help us improve it: which features were used, along with a few setting values such as the fact that you turned the reminder on and the time you set it to. It never includes the text of your entries or the content of your hashtags. See the Privacy Policy for details.
