Frequently asked questions

Can I write an entry for yesterday?

No. You can only record the current day, and there is no date picker. Past entries can be read back from the calendar, but their content cannot be changed. The app is built around remembering one thing while the day is still yours, rather than filling in a backlog later. An entry for the current day can still be edited or deleted after you save it.

Can I record more than one good deed a day?

One a day. Writing a second one replaces that day's entry rather than adding to it.

Is there a limit on the length of an entry?

Up to 100 characters. The remaining count is shown under the text field. Hashtags are separate from the text, and you can add as many as you like.

I am not getting notifications

First check that "Daily reminder" is switched on under Settings > Reminder in the app. Then check that notifications are allowed for the app under Settings > Notifications > One Good Deed on your device. If they are not, the app's settings screen shows an "Open notification settings" button.

Note that reminders are scheduled about two weeks ahead at a time. If you do not open the app for more than two weeks, the schedule runs out and reminders stop, so please open the app once to refill it. If you have already recorded an entry for the day, that day's reminder is skipped.

Can I choose which days of the week to be reminded?

No. The reminder is once a day, every day, at the time you set. You can change the time under Settings > Reminder > Time.

Can I move my entries to a new device?

Yes. Use Settings > Data > Export JSON to write your entries out as a file (JSON format), which you can then save or send from the share sheet. On the new device, use Settings > Data > Import JSON to load that file.

Importing replaces rather than merges. Everything already on that device is replaced by the contents of the file. Please take care if you have already recorded a few days on the new device.

Are my entries synced across devices?

No. Your entries, hashtags, and settings are all stored only on your device, and nothing is kept in iCloud or any other cloud service. No account is required. To carry entries between devices, use the export and import described above.

What happens to my data if I delete the app?

Every entry stored on the device is erased along with it, and there is no way to recover it. If you want to keep anything, export it as a file before deleting.

Can I change the colours?

Yes. Under Settings > Theme you can choose between four palettes — spring, summer, autumn, and winter. Turning on "Follow the season" switches them automatically according to the month. Light and dark appearance can be chosen on the same screen.

Does the home screen icon change with the season too?

Not by default. The icon only changes when you turn on Settings > Theme > Change the app icon too, and it then follows whichever seasonal palette you are using. The palette and the icon are separate settings. Note that iOS shows a confirmation dialog each time the icon changes.

I would like to try the app first

Tap "Explore with sample data" on the last page of the introduction, or use Settings > Data > Add sample data, to load example entries and try every screen. Sample data can be removed again from the same screen with "Remove sample data", and entries you wrote yourself are left untouched.

Can I show an entry to someone?

Yes. There is a share button when you open a day from the calendar, and another on the recap screen. It opens the iOS share sheet, so you can pass the text to mail, messages, social apps, and so on. There is no in-app feature for sharing entries with other users.

Where can I see my streak?

The Recap tab shows your current streak, your longest streak, and your total number of entries, along with a breakdown by hashtag and the milestones you have reached for days in a row and entries recorded.

About the supporter plan

What is the supporter plan?

It is a way to support the development of this app. Nothing is restricted if you do not subscribe. Recording, the calendar, the recap, export, and the colour themes are all free to use.

Can I sign up now?

Not yet — it is still in preparation. Tapping "Become a supporter" in the app will not let you subscribe at this time. We will announce it on this page when it opens.

Requirements

iPhone running iOS 18.0 or later. iPad is not supported. The app is available in Japanese and English.
