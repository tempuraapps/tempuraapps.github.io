Frequently asked questions

Can I write an entry for yesterday?

No. You can only record the current day, and there is no date picker. Past entries can be read back from the calendar, but their content cannot be changed. The app is built around remembering one thing while the day is still fresh, rather than filling in a backlog later. An entry for the current day can still be edited or deleted after you save it.

Can I record more than one good deed a day?

One a day. Writing a second one replaces that day's entry rather than adding to it.

Is there a limit on the length of an entry?

Up to 100 characters. A count is shown under the text field, as "12 / 100". Hashtags are separate from the text, and you can add as many as you like.

I am not getting notifications

First check that "Daily reminder" is switched on under Settings > Reminder in the app. Then check that notifications are allowed for the app under Settings > Notifications > One Good Deed on your device. If they are not, the app's settings screen shows an "Open notification settings" button.

Note that reminders are scheduled about two weeks ahead at a time. If you do not open the app for more than two weeks, the schedule runs out and reminders stop, so please open the app once so it can reschedule them. If you have already recorded an entry for the day, that day's reminder is skipped.

Can I choose which days of the week to be reminded?

No. The reminder is once a day, every day, at the time you set. You can change the time under Settings > Reminder > Time.

Can I move my entries to a new device?

This is a Pro feature. Use Settings > Data > Export JSON to write your entries out as a file (JSON format), which you can then save or send from the share sheet. On the new device, use Settings > Data > Import JSON to load that file.

Importing replaces rather than merges. Everything already on that device is replaced by the contents of the file. Please take care if you have already recorded a few days on the new device.

Are my entries synced across devices?

No. The text of your entries and your hashtags are stored only on your device and are never sent to a server. Your settings are kept on the device too, although a few of them appear in the usage statistics described below. No account is required. To carry entries between devices, use the export and import described above (a Pro feature).

The app does send anonymous usage statistics to help us improve it — which features were used, along with counts. That includes a few setting values, such as the fact that you turned the reminder on and the time you set it to. It never includes the text of your entries or the content of your hashtags. See the Privacy Policy for details. Also note that if you have iCloud Backup switched on for the device itself, the app's data is included in that backup.

What happens to my data if I delete the app?

Every entry stored on the device is erased along with it, and we cannot recover it for you. If you want to keep anything, export it as a file before deleting.

Can I delete a past entry?

Not individually. Only the current day's entry can be edited or deleted. To clear everything, use Settings > Reset > Delete all entries.

Can I change the colours?

Yes. Under Settings > Theme you can choose between four palettes — spring, summer, autumn, and winter. Turning on "Follow the season" switches them automatically according to the month. Light and dark appearance can be chosen on the same screen.

Does the home screen icon change with the season too?

Not by default. The icon only changes when you turn on Settings > Theme > Change the app icon too, and it then follows whichever seasonal palette you are using. The palette and the icon are separate settings. Note that iOS shows an alert each time the icon changes to tell you it has changed.

I would like to try the app first

Tap "Explore with sample data" on the last page of the introduction, or use Settings > Data > Add sample data, to load example entries and try every screen. Sample data can be removed again from the same screen with "Remove sample data", and entries you wrote yourself are left untouched.

Can I show an entry to someone?

Yes. There is a share button on today's entry on the home screen, and another when you open a day from the calendar; both pass the text of that entry to the iOS share sheet, so you can send it to mail, messages, social apps, and so on. The recap screen also has a share button, but that one shares a summary of the month ("12 good deeds in July") rather than the text of any entry. There is no in-app feature for sharing entries with other users.

Where can I see my streak?

The Recap tab shows your current streak, your longest streak, and your total number of entries, along with a breakdown by hashtag and the milestones you have reached for days in a row. Milestones for your total number of entries are not listed there; they appear as a celebration when you reach one.

Does my streak reset if I have not written today yet?

No. As long as you wrote yesterday, your streak keeps showing while today is still unwritten, and writing today adds one to it. If you miss a whole day, though, the streak breaks and starts again from one.

About Pro

Can I keep using the app for free?

Yes. Writing your good deed for the day, reading back any past entry from the calendar, the daily reminder, and sharing an entry are all free. Nothing you need in order to keep the habit is behind the paywall.

What does Pro add?

  • The recap — the breakdown of your good deeds, your streak milestones, and every past month. On the free plan you can see this month's count along with your current, longest, and total figures.
  • Unlimited hashtags — the free plan allows three. Tags you have already created can be reused as often as you like.
  • Seasonal palettes and app icons — pick spring, summer, autumn, or winter. On the free plan the palette still follows the season automatically.
  • Dark mode
  • Export and import as JSON

How much does it cost?

Pricing is set per storefront by the App Store; in Japan it is ¥300 a month or ¥3,000 a year (the yearly plan works out to two months free). Payment is handled through the App Store, and you can cancel at any time from Settings > Manage subscription in the app or from your iOS settings.

Requirements

iPhone running iOS 18.0 or later. iPad is not supported. The app is available in Japanese and English.
