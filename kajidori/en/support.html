Frequently asked questions

Where do I start?

The first time you open the app, you’re asked to add the people you share chores with. Give each a name and a color — up to two on the free plan — then move on to choosing chore templates. Check off the presets you want and you’re set. From there, lay a few chores onto this week’s or next week’s schedule and you can start using it right away.

How is the assignee for a chore decided?

You can pick a member when you schedule a chore, or leave it “Undecided.” Undecided chores stand out on Today and on the board, so you can still settle it together on the day. We haven’t built a fairness score or a leaderboard. Making the next chore easy to schedule matters more here than getting a perfect split from the start.

I want chores to repeat every week

Choose the days it repeats when you schedule a chore, and it’s generated automatically from today out to as far as the view shows (up to 13 weeks). Editing or deleting always asks whether you mean “just this one” or “this and every one after,” so changing a single occurrence is safe. To skip just one occurrence that came from a repeat, choose “Skip this one” instead of deleting it — that keeps the row in place so the next occurrence still gets generated.

Does the timer keep running in the background?

Yes. Elapsed time is calculated from the moment you started, so closing the app, force-quitting it, or restarting your phone doesn’t lose anything — reopening the app brings the running session back automatically with the elapsed time intact. If a session runs past 24 hours, a notice appears (it is never ended automatically).

How does the step guide work?

Add steps to a chore and the running screen always shows “the current step” and “the next step” in large type. Check one off to move to the next, or open the step list from the bookmark icon to jump to any step directly. Work steps and waiting steps are shown differently.

I want to correct the actual time on a completed record

You can edit the actual minutes by hand on the record screen. If it’s far from the estimate, a note flags it as a possible outlier and lets you choose whether it should count toward the next estimate (it starts out excluded). You can change that later from the record’s detail view too.

How is the next estimate calculated?

A chore with no history uses the estimate you set when you created it. After one or two records, it becomes a weighted average of that estimate and what actually happened; from three records on, it switches to the average of the three most recent. Records treated as outliers are excluded by default, so one unusually long day won’t throw off the estimate.

When do notifications arrive?

A morning notification (8:00 AM by default) lists how many chores are due today and their total estimated time; an evening one (9:00 PM by default) covers tomorrow’s plan and anything left today. Both give only a count and a total time — never the chores themselves. Nothing arrives on a day with nothing to report. Turn each kind on or off in Settings.

What happens when I archive a member?

Any chore they were assigned to from today onward reverts to Undecided. Past records keep their name exactly as it was, so the history of who did what is never lost. The last remaining member can’t be archived.

What happens when I delete (archive) a chore template?

An archived chore stops appearing in new schedules, but anything already scheduled for the future can still be run as normal. Past records keep the chore’s name exactly as it was.

Does my data sync across devices?

It doesn’t. Members, chore templates, schedules, records, and notes are stored only on the device you use. With Kajidori Pro, you can export and import as a JSON file from Settings. The exported file carries member and chore names as you wrote them, so use it to move to another device or to keep a copy. Importing replaces your current data (your existing data is set aside automatically first).

What happens to my data if I delete the app?

Members, chore templates, schedules, and records stored on your device are removed with it. If you want to keep them, export from Settings > Data (Pro) first.

I want to delete all my data

“Delete all data” in Settings clears everything on your device at once. It can’t be undone, so export a copy first with Pro if you might want it later.

I’d like to try it with sample data

Adding sample data from Settings fills the app with members, chore templates, and a schedule so you can try it out. Remove it any time from the same place.

About Kajidori Pro

What does Pro unlock?

Five things.

  • Add members up to 6
  • Create as many chore templates as you like
  • Look back through your full history
  • Switch to Dark or Automatic appearance
  • Export and import your data as JSON

Everything else — scheduling (weekly and monthly), repeats, the timer and step guide, records and estimate learning, the last 30 days of history, notifications, adding sample data, and deleting all data — is free.

What does it cost?

¥300 per month or ¥3,000 per year in Japan (prices vary by country or region; the App Store shows your local price). The yearly plan costs exactly ten months at the monthly rate. There is no free trial. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase, and renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends. The renewal charge is taken within 24 hours of the end of the period.

I purchased but Pro isn’t showing

Try “Restore purchases” on the Pro screen. You need to be signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you purchased with. If it still doesn’t appear, please get in touch.

I want to cancel my subscription

Cancellation happens on the App Store side. Open Settings > your name > Subscriptions on your iPhone, choose Kajidori, and select “Cancel Subscription.” The same screen opens from “Manage subscription” under Settings > Plan in the app.

After cancelling, Pro features stay available until the period you have paid for ends. Cancelling on its own does not produce a prorated refund (see “I’d like a refund” below).

I want to switch between monthly and yearly

Switch from the same Settings > your name > Subscriptions screen. The two Kajidori plans sit at the same level, so a switch takes effect at your next renewal date — it does not swap immediately, and no prorated refund is involved. Until then, the plan you are paying for continues.

What happens to my members and data if I cancel?

Nothing is deleted. Your members, chore templates, and every schedule and record stay exactly as they were. Only the five things above stop: you can no longer add members or chore templates beyond the free limits, the appearance reverts to Light, and records older than 30 days drop out of view (the records themselves stay, and come back the moment you subscribe again). Subscribe again and it all comes back as it was.

I want to check my renewal date

Open Settings > your name > Subscriptions to see the next renewal date and amount. Auto-renewal continues unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends.

I’d like a refund

Payments are handled by Apple, and refund decisions are Apple’s. Please apply at reportaproblem.apple.com. We are not able to process refunds ourselves.

Requirements

iPhone running iOS 18.0 or later. iPad is not supported. The app is available in Japanese and English.
