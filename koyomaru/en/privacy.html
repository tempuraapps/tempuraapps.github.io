Koyomaru Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

About the app · Support · 日本語

Koyomaru ("the App") is provided by TempuraApps ("we"). This policy explains how the App handles user information.

1. Information we collect

The App does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. The events the App shows and edits are the ones stored in your iPhone calendars (section 5). The App never sends the contents of your events — titles, dates and times, notes, and so on — to servers operated by us or by third parties. The only exceptions are usage measurement to improve the App (section 3) and checking purchase status (section 4), for which limited information is sent to external services. Neither includes the contents of your events or any text you typed.

2. Accounts and network use

No account registration is required to use the App. We do not keep your events in an account or on a server of our own. The App does, however, communicate with a limited set of external services (PostHog, RevenueCat, and Apple’s App Store) for usage measurement (section 3) and purchase status checks (section 4).

If you have added accounts such as iCloud or Google to Calendar on your iPhone, your events sync with the provider of that account. That syncing is done by your iPhone’s Calendar, not by the App. Events you add, change, or delete in the App sync the same way.

3. Usage analytics (PostHog)

To improve quality and features, the App measures anonymous usage with the analytics service PostHog. What is collected is limited to the fact that one of the actions below took place, together with the kind of option involved.

  • Installing, opening, or updating the App, and moving it to the foreground or background
  • Finishing the first-run introduction, and the result of the calendar access request (granted, denied, restricted, add-only, or not yet answered)
  • Changing the month (by swiping or with the Today button)
  • Tapping a date to open that day’s list (where it was opened from, and a bucket for the number of events that day: 0, 1–3, 4–9, or 10 or more)
  • Opening an event’s details (whether it is all-day, whether it repeats, and whether it can be edited)
  • Opening the event editor (new or existing event, and where it was opened from), and how it ended (saved, cancelled, or deleted)
  • Changing the day the week starts on (Sunday or Monday)
  • Changing the appearance (Auto, Light, or Dark)

Nothing beyond this is sent. We never send event titles, dates and times, or notes, calendar names, label colors, search terms, or input history — the values that can be sent are limited to numbers, true/false, and a fixed set of options, so there is no mechanism for carrying text you typed. Analytics features that could pick up event contents — rage-click detection, which would send on-screen text, and Surveys — are disabled.

Technical information automatically attached by PostHog may also be included: the App version, your OS type and version, your device type, and the App's display language. Your time zone is stripped before sending, because it can be used to infer your approximate region. IP addresses are configured to be discarded on the analytics side, and we have specified that no region or location be inferred from them. Finer locations such as your city or your coordinates are never collected. None of this is intended to identify a specific individual, and none of it is linked to an account. The data is stored in the EU region.

Each record sent carries an anonymous identifier created for that installation of the App. It exists so that actions from the same installation can be read as one thread. It is not tied to your name, email address, account, or any hardware identifier provided by the device, and we do not use it to identify you. Deleting and reinstalling the App produces a new one.

We have no way to connect that identifier to you as a person. For that reason we cannot act on requests to retrieve or delete one user's data individually. If you wish to stop the measurement entirely, please delete the App; nothing is sent after it is removed.

The App does not serve advertising or perform advertising tracking (IDFA and similar) of any kind. Nor do we use the information collected for advertising purposes, or provide or sell it to third parties.

4. Purchase status (RevenueCat)

The current version has no paid features that can be purchased. The App includes the subscription infrastructure service RevenueCat in preparation for offering paid features in the future, and it connects to RevenueCat at launch to check purchase status. This connection uses an anonymous identifier and is not linked to personal information such as your name or email address. It does not include the contents of your events. Please note that RevenueCat is a service provided by a company based in the United States.

5. Access to your iPhone calendars

If you allow it, the App has full access to the calendars on your iPhone. It reads the events in every calendar on your iPhone (title, dates and times, whether all-day, repeat, alert settings, and notes), along with each calendar’s name and color. These are used on your device for the month view, each day’s list, event details, and search.

The App writes to your iPhone calendars when you add, change, duplicate, or delete an event in the App, and when you import an .ics file (section 7). New events are saved to the calendar set as the default on your iPhone.

The App never sends the contents of the events it reads to servers operated by us or by third parties. You can revoke access at any time in your iPhone’s Settings (once revoked, the App can no longer show your events).

Birthdays are shown by reading the Birthdays calendar that your iPhone builds from your contacts; the App itself never accesses your contacts. The App does not ask for access to Contacts, Location, Photos, or notifications. Event alerts are delivered by your iPhone’s Calendar.

6. Information stored on your device

The App stores the following information only on your device. None of it is sent to us.

  • The label color chosen for each event (pairs of the identifier your iPhone assigns to the event and a color number)
  • Display settings (appearance, the day the week starts on, button position, Show Birthdays, Give Busy Weeks More Room, and whether the lock is on)
  • Input history (titles of events you saved, up to 30)
  • Identifiers of events imported from .ics files (so the same event isn’t added twice)
  • The month and date you were last viewing

If you use a whole-device backup (such as iCloud Backup), your iPhone may include this information in that backup.

7. Importing from another calendar (.ics files)

“Move from Another Calendar” in Settings reads the .ics file you choose on your device and adds the events in it (title, dates and times, repeat, and notes) to your iPhone calendar. Neither the file nor its contents is sent to servers operated by us or by third parties. The added events become events in your iPhone calendar and, as described in section 2, sync according to your account settings.

8. App lock (Face ID, Touch ID, passcode)

If you turn on the lock in Settings (for example “Lock with Face ID”), the App uses your iPhone’s authentication to confirm it’s you. All the App receives is whether authentication succeeded. The App cannot access your face or fingerprint data or your passcode, and never stores or sends them.

9. Sharing with third parties

We do not provide or sell your information to third parties for advertising purposes. Information sent to external services (PostHog and RevenueCat) for the measurement and purchase status checks described in sections 3 and 4 is handled under each service’s own privacy policy. You can read them here: PostHog, RevenueCat.

10. Deleting your data

Your events belong to your iPhone calendars. Deleting an event from its details in the App deletes it from your iPhone calendar, and it also disappears from synced accounts and other calendar apps. Deleting the App leaves your events in your iPhone calendars.

The on-device information in section 6 is erased when you delete the App. Because we have no way to connect the analytics records in section 3 to you as a person, we cannot delete them individually.

11. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy as features are added or changed. If a change is significant, we will announce it on this page.
