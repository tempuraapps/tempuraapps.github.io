Koyomaru Support

About the app · Privacy Policy · 日本語

Koyomaru lays out the events in your iPhone calendars in an easy-to-read month view. Below are answers to the questions we hear most, such as how to add calendars and how to move from another app. The basic gestures are also listed in the app under Settings > About > “How to Use” (“Help” just below it opens this page). If something isn’t answered here, please get in touch.

Frequently asked questions

Showing your calendars

I want to see my Google Calendar events

On your iPhone, go to Settings > Apps > Calendar > Calendar Accounts and add your Google account, and its events show up in Koyomaru as they are. (If you can’t find it, type “Calendar” into the search field in Settings.) You don’t need to sign in to Google inside Koyomaru. The same goes for accounts from other calendar services. Your iPhone handles syncing for those events.

I added my account, but my events don’t appear

If events still don’t appear after you add an account on your iPhone, check the following.

  • Calendars are off for the account — In Settings > Apps > Calendar > Calendar Accounts, select the account and turn on its calendars.
  • Some calendars are missing — Shared calendars may need to be turned on for syncing to your iPhone on the service’s side.
  • Just added the account — Syncing can take a little while. Wait a bit, then open the app again.

My events don’t show up / it says “No Access to Calendars” or “Can’t View Events”

In your iPhone’s Settings, set Koyomaru’s access to Calendars to “Full Access.” You can get there with “Open iPhone Settings” in Koyomaru. With “Add Events Only,” the app can’t read your events.

I want to see holidays

If your iPhone calendars include a holiday calendar (such as a national holidays calendar), holidays appear as red labels and their dates turn red. Koyomaru treats all-day events as holidays when they are in a subscribed calendar whose name contains “holiday” (or “祝日” in Japanese). Every all-day event in that calendar is treated the same way, so observances that aren’t days off also appear in red. If holidays don’t appear, check that a holiday calendar has been added to your iPhone calendars. Koyomaru has no holiday data of its own.

I want to hide (or show) birthdays

Switch it with Settings > Display > “Show Birthdays” (on by default). The birthdays shown come from the Birthdays calendar your iPhone builds from your contacts.

Can I see rokuyo (the traditional Japanese six-day cycle)?

Only when the app is displayed in Japanese. There, rokuyo appear next to each date and at the top of each day’s list (from January 31, 1900 to December 31, 2096), and can be turned off under Settings > Display (on by default). When the app is in English, neither rokuyo nor that setting is shown.

Do events added in other apps show up?

Yes. Koyomaru shows every event in your iPhone calendars. There’s no option to choose which calendars to show.

What does “+3” mean?

It’s the number of events that didn’t fit on that day. Tap the date to see every event for that day.

How do I move to another month?

Swipe left or right for the next or previous month. Tap the month at the top, or the calendar button at the bottom, to open “Go to Month,” where you step through the years and pick one of the 12 months. Tap Today to return to today’s month. Each time you launch the app, it starts on today’s month (it also returns there when you come back after 30 minutes or more away).

Adding and editing events

How do I add an event?

Tap the + at the bottom right, or touch and hold a date, and choose “Timed Event” or “All-Day Event.” You can enter a title, Location or Note, color, all-day, start and end, repeat, alert, and notes.

What goes in “Location or Note”?

It’s a single line under the title for a place or a short note. It’s saved as the event’s location in your iPhone calendar, so other calendar apps show it as the location. A location entered in another app shows up here, too. It appears in the day’s list, the event details, and search results, not on the month labels. There’s no map or address search.

Which calendar do new events go into?

The calendar set as the Default Calendar on your iPhone (if that calendar can’t be written to, another calendar that can). You can’t choose a different calendar inside Koyomaru. Saved events also show up in other calendar apps. To change it, go to Settings > Apps > Calendar on your iPhone.

I want to change a label color

In the event editor, tap the Color row and choose Match Calendar or one of 20 colors. The color is saved only in Koyomaru and doesn’t change how other calendar apps show the event. Events set to Match Calendar are shown in the color closest to their calendar’s color.

I want to move an event to another day

Touch and hold a label in the month view, then drag it to another day to move it there. You can also touch and hold an event in a day’s list, or the title area at the top of an event’s details, and drag it onto a date the same way (except for details opened from Search). The time and length don’t change. Right after, a bar at the bottom offers “Undo” and “Duplicate Instead,” which keeps the event on the original day as well. Pause near the left or right edge of the month to go to the previous or next month. For a repeating event, you choose “Move This Event Only,” “Move All Future Events,” or “Duplicate to This Day” when you drop it, and Undo isn’t offered if you move it. An event with invitees asks for confirmation before it moves, and Undo and Duplicate Instead aren’t offered afterward.

Some events can’t be edited

Holidays, birthdays, and events in read-only calendars (such as subscribed calendars) can’t be edited, moved, or deleted.

What happens when I change or delete a repeating event?

When saving or deleting, you choose whether it applies to this event only or to all future events. If you change the repeat rule itself, the change applies to all future events. The repeat options are Daily, Weekly, Every 2 weeks, Monthly, Yearly, and “Custom…,” where you can set the interval, the days of the week, a day of the month or a rule like “the second Tuesday,” and an end (a date or a number of times). A repeat rule made in another app that Koyomaru can’t express is kept as it is (“Keep Current Setting”).

Will I get alerts?

If you set an alert on an event (At time of event, 5, 10, or 30 minutes before, 1 hour before, or 1 day before), it arrives as an iPhone Calendar notification. Koyomaru never sends notifications itself and never asks for notification permission.

If I delete an event, does it disappear from other apps too?

Yes. Koyomaru works directly with the events in your iPhone calendars.

Can I get back an event I deleted by mistake?

When you swipe left on an event in a day’s list and tap “Delete,” “Undo” appears at the bottom for a few seconds (this delete doesn’t ask for confirmation). For a repeating event you choose the range first, and Undo isn’t offered. Undo isn’t offered for events with invitees, either. “Delete” in the event details and “Delete Event” in the event editor ask for confirmation and can’t be undone.

I want to find an event

Use Search at the bottom of the screen to search by title and by Location or Note. It covers events from 10 years back to 10 years ahead, and results are split into “Upcoming” (soonest first) and “Past” (most recent first). If there are many, it shows the 1,000 closest to today, so add words to narrow them down. Holidays don’t appear in search results.

Display settings

I want weeks to start on Monday

Choose Sunday or Monday in Settings > Display > “Week Starts On.”

How are week numbers counted?

With weeks starting on Monday, Koyomaru follows ISO 8601 (week 1 is the week with the year’s first Thursday). With weeks starting on Sunday, week 1 is the week that contains January 1.

I want to use dark mode

Choose Auto, Light, or Dark in Settings > Theme > “Appearance.”

Busy weeks are cramped and hard to read

Turn on Settings > Display > “Expand Busy Weeks” to make weeks with many events taller.

I want to use it left-handed

Set Settings > Display > “Button Position” to Left, and the buttons for adding an event and “Go to Month” move to the left, while Search moves to the right.

Can I use 24-hour time?

Koyomaru follows your iPhone. You can switch it with “24-Hour Time” in Settings > General > Date & Time on your iPhone.

App lock

I want to lock the app

Turn on the first switch under Settings > Lock & Data (for example “Lock with Face ID”; you authenticate once when turning it on). The app then asks for Face ID or Touch ID when you open it or come back to it, and you can unlock with your iPhone passcode if those aren’t available. While locked, your events don’t show in the app switcher, either.

I can’t turn on the lock / it turned itself off

The lock can’t be used unless your iPhone has a passcode set. If you remove the passcode later, the lock turns itself off so you aren’t locked out.

Switching apps and your data

I want to move from another calendar app

Events in accounts such as Google Calendar show up as soon as you add the account in your iPhone’s Settings (see “I want to see my Google Calendar events” above). For events that exist only inside the other app, export them from that app as an .ics file (see “How to export an .ics file”), then import it in Koyomaru with Settings > Lock & Data > “Import from Other Calendars” > “Import .ics File.”

How to export an .ics file

Each app exports differently. Look in that app’s settings or menus for an item such as “Export” or “.ics.” Save the exported file in the Files app, then choose it with “Import .ics File” in Koyomaru (one file at a time). If you get a .zip file, tap it in the Files app to open it, then choose the .ics file inside. Events in an app that can’t export to .ics can’t be moved to Koyomaru. Events synced through an account, such as Google Calendar, don’t need to be exported; they appear once you add the account on your iPhone.

What comes in with an .ics import?

Titles, dates and times, whether an event is all-day, locations (they go into “Location or Note”), notes, and repeats. Alerts aren’t imported. Before anything is added, you can check the number of events, their date range, and the calendar they’ll go into. Imported events go into the same calendar as new events (your iPhone’s Default Calendar). There’s no way to undo an import all at once.

Do repeating events come in as repeating?

Repeats that Koyomaru can express (including “Custom…”) come in as repeating events, along with their interval, days of the week, rules like “the second Tuesday,” and an end date or count. Rules Koyomaru can’t express, and repeats with individual occurrences removed or changed, are added as their first occurrence only, and the confirmation screen shows how many are affected. A repeating event whose first occurrence itself was removed or changed can’t be added; the number of those is shown as well.

What happens if I import the same file again?

Events that already exist in your iPhone calendar (same title, dates, and times) aren’t added again. If some failed partway through, importing again adds only the rest.

What happens to my events when I change iPhones?

Your events live in your iPhone calendars (accounts such as iCloud or Google), so they appear on your new iPhone when you use the same accounts. Label colors, settings, and recent titles are stored on the device, so they may not carry over.

If I delete the app, are my events deleted?

No. Your events stay in your iPhone calendars. What is removed is what Koyomaru keeps on the device: label colors, settings, and recent titles.

Does it sync with my other devices?

Events sync through your iPhone’s calendar accounts. Koyomaru has no sync of its own.

Does it cost anything?

The current version has no in-app purchases.

Requirements

iPhone running iOS 18.0 or later. iPad is not supported. The app is available in Japanese and English.
