About the app · Privacy Policy · 日本語

Koyomaru lays out the events in your iPhone calendars in an easy-to-read month view. Below are answers to the questions we hear most, such as how to add calendars and how to move from another app. If something isn’t answered here, please get in touch.

Frequently asked questions

Showing your calendars

I want to see my Google Calendar events

Add your Google account to Calendar in your iPhone’s Settings, and its events show up in Koyomaru as they are. In Settings, open the Calendar item and add the account from its accounts section. (If you can’t find the Calendar item, type “Calendar” into the search field in Settings.) You don’t need to sign in to Google inside Koyomaru. The same goes for Outlook and other services. Your iPhone handles syncing for those events.

My events don’t show up / it says “No Access to Calendars”

In your iPhone’s Settings, set Koyomaru’s access to Calendars to “Full Access.” You can get there with “Open Settings” in Koyomaru. With “Add Events Only,” the app can’t read your events.

I want to see holidays

If your iPhone calendars include a holiday calendar (such as a national holidays calendar), holidays appear as red labels and their dates turn red. Everything in that calendar is treated the same way, so observances that aren’t days off also appear in red. A holiday calendar is recognized by the word “holiday” in its name. If they don’t appear, check in the iPhone Calendar app that the holiday calendar is turned on. Koyomaru has no holiday data of its own.

I want to hide (or show) birthdays

Switch it with Settings > Display > “Show Birthdays” (on by default). The birthdays shown come from the Birthdays calendar your iPhone builds from your contacts.

Do events added in other apps show up?

Yes. Koyomaru shows every event in your iPhone calendars. There’s no option to choose which calendars to show.

What does “+3” mean?

It’s the number of events that didn’t fit on that day. Tap the date to see every event for that day.

Adding and editing events

How do I add an event?

Tap the + at the bottom right, or touch and hold a date, and choose “Timed Event” or “All-Day Event.” You can enter a title, label color, all-day, start and end, repeat, alert, and notes.

Which calendar do new events go into?

The calendar set as the Default Calendar on your iPhone (if that calendar can’t be written to, another calendar that can). You can’t choose a different calendar inside Koyomaru. Saved events also show up in other calendar apps.

I want to change a label color

Choose from 20 colors in the event editor. The color is saved only in Koyomaru and doesn’t change how other calendar apps show the event. Events without a chosen color are shown in the color closest to their calendar’s color.

Some events can’t be edited

Holidays, birthdays, and events in read-only calendars (such as subscribed calendars) can’t be edited or deleted.

What happens when I change or delete a repeating event?

When saving or deleting, you choose whether it applies to this event only or to all future events. If you change the repeat rule itself, the change applies to all future events. The repeat options are Daily, Weekly, Every 2 weeks, Monthly, and Yearly. A more detailed repeat rule made in another app is kept as it is (“Keep current”).

Will I get alerts?

If you set an alert on an event (At time of event, 5, 10, or 30 minutes before, 1 hour before, or 1 day before), it arrives as an iPhone Calendar notification. Koyomaru never sends notifications itself and never asks for notification permission.

If I delete an event, does it disappear from other apps too?

Yes. Koyomaru works directly with the events in your iPhone calendars.

I want to find an event

Use Search at the bottom of the screen to search by title. It covers events from one year back to two years ahead. Holidays don’t appear in search results.

Display settings

I want weeks to start on Monday

Choose Sunday or Monday in Settings > Display > “Week Starts On.”

How are week numbers counted?

With weeks starting on Monday, Koyomaru follows ISO 8601 (week 1 is the week with the year’s first Thursday). With weeks starting on Sunday, week 1 is the week that contains January 1.

I want to use dark mode

Choose Auto, Light, or Dark in Settings > Theme.

Busy weeks are cramped and hard to read

Turn on Settings > Display > “Expand Busy Weeks” to make weeks with many events taller.

I want to use it left-handed

Set Settings > Display > “Button Position” to Left, and the buttons for adding an event and jumping to a date move to the left.

Can I use 24-hour time?

Koyomaru follows the 24-Hour Time setting on your iPhone.

App lock

I want to lock the app

Turn on the switch under Settings > Lock (for example “Lock with Face ID”; you authenticate once when turning it on). The app then asks for Face ID or Touch ID when you open it or come back to it, and you can unlock with your iPhone passcode if those aren’t available. While locked, your events don’t show in the app switcher, either.

I can’t turn on the lock / it turned itself off

The lock can’t be used unless your iPhone has a passcode set. If you remove the passcode later, the lock turns itself off so you aren’t locked out.

Switching apps and your data

I want to move from another calendar app

Events in accounts such as Google or Outlook show up as soon as you add the account in your iPhone’s Settings (see “I want to see my Google Calendar events” above). For events that exist only inside the other app, export them from that app as an .ics file, then import it in Koyomaru with Settings > Data > “Move from Another Calendar” > “Import .ics File.”

What comes in with an .ics import?

Titles, dates and times, whether an event is all-day, notes, and repeats. Locations and alerts aren’t imported. You can check the number of events before they are added. Imported events go into your iPhone’s Default Calendar.

Do repeating events come in as repeating?

Simple repeats with no end — daily, weekly, every 2 weeks, monthly, or yearly — come in as repeating events (including weekly repeats on one specified weekday). Repeats with an end date or a count, several weekdays, or rules like “the second Tuesday” are added as their first occurrence only, and the confirmation screen shows how many are affected.

What happens if I import the same file again?

Events that were already imported aren’t added again. If some failed partway through, importing again adds only the rest.

What happens to my events when I change iPhones?

Your events live in your iPhone calendars (accounts such as iCloud or Google), so they appear on your new iPhone when you use the same accounts. Label colors, settings, and recent titles are stored on the device, so they may not carry over.

If I delete the app, are my events deleted?

No. Your events stay in your iPhone calendars. What is removed is what Koyomaru keeps on the device: label colors, settings, and recent titles.

Does it sync with my other devices?

Events sync through your iPhone’s calendar accounts. Koyomaru has no sync of its own.

Does it cost anything?

The current version has no in-app purchases.

Requirements

iPhone running iOS 18.0 or later. iPad is not supported. The app is available in Japanese and English.
