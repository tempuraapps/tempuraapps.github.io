Walk somewhere new.

As you walk, the streets you cover are drawn onto the map. Once you notice you always turn at the same corner, head somewhere you haven't been. No account, no ads.

iOS 18.0 or later / iPhone

Four tools for an ordinary walk

  • Record a walk

    Tap start and your distance and route build up as you go. Recording continues with the screen off and the phone in your pocket. Tap "End walk" when you're done.

  • Where you walked

    Everywhere you've walked accumulates on the map. The streets you assumed you'd covered stay blank — and stay obvious.

  • Today's direction

    Once a day, the app suggests a direction you haven't walked yet. Leave home without a plan and your walk still has a heading.

  • Find somewhere to go

    Choose your destination — and your starting point — by search or on the map. Save the ones you like as spots and they become landmarks for the next walk.

Export and import your records as JSON.

Where you walked stays on your device

Your walks, routes, the map of where you have walked, spots and notes are stored only on your device. Nothing syncs to another device and nothing is sent to our servers. The exception is showing a map or searching for a place, where the visible area or your search terms go to Apple's map services. See the Privacy Policy for details.
