Frequently asked questions

My walk stops being recorded partway through

Check that location access is set to "While Using the App" under Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > MichiAruki. Recording is designed to continue while the screen is off, but iOS may suspend an app to save power. Turning off Low Power Mode, and avoiding other location-heavy apps while you walk, makes recording more reliable.

Can I put the phone in my pocket with the screen off?

Yes. Once you start a walk, location use continues with the screen off and your distance and route keep building. It continues until you stop recording, so please tap "End walk" when your walk is over.

Do I have to allow location access to use the app?

No. The covered-area map, spots, past walks, and settings all work without it. Location access is needed only to record a new walk and to suggest destinations near you. You can change the permission at any time in Settings.

Is my recorded data synced across devices?

No. Your walks, routes, covered-area map, favourite spots, and notes are all stored only on your device. To move them to a new device, use the export feature in Settings to save a file (JSON format) and import it on the new device.

Is where I walk ever sent to a server?

No. Location data and routes stay on your device. The one exception is destination search: when you look for a spot such as a park or café, the surrounding coordinates and your search terms are sent to Apple's map service. That does not pass through our servers and we do not store it. See the Privacy Policy for details.

What happens to my data if I delete the app?

Your walks, covered-area map, spots, and notes stored on the device are erased along with it. If you want to keep anything, export it as a file from Settings before deleting.

I would like to try the app without going for a walk

Tap "Try it with sample data" on the last onboarding page, or use Settings > Data > Add sample data, to load sample walks and try every screen. Sample data can be removed again from the same screen.

About MichiAruki Pro (the Supporter Plan)

MichiAruki Pro is shown inside the app as the "Supporter Plan" — the two names refer to the same subscription.

What does Pro unlock?

Re-drawing the daily direction (you can keep up to three per day) and exporting your report as a shareable image. Drawing a direction itself is always free, once per day. Nothing else is restricted.

I purchased Pro but it is not active

Try "Restore purchases" in the app. You need to be signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you used to purchase. If it still does not activate, please contact us at the address above.

I want to cancel my subscription

Cancellation is handled by the App Store. Open Settings > your name > Subscriptions, select MichiAruki, and choose "Cancel Subscription". "Manage subscription" in the app opens the same screen.

After cancelling, Pro features remain available until the period you have already paid for ends. Partial refunds are not issued.

I want to check my renewal date

Under Settings > your name > Subscriptions you can see the next renewal date and amount. Auto-renewal continues unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends.

I would like a refund

Payment is handled by Apple, and refund decisions are Apple's to make. Please apply at reportaproblem.apple.com. We are not able to process refunds ourselves.

Requirements

iPhone running iOS 18.0 or later. iPad is not supported. The app is available in Japanese and English.
