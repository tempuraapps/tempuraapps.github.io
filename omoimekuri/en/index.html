OmoiMekuri

The photo that matters,
comes to find you today.

Add photos to a theme, and one photo arrives each day. Photos you mark “See again” come back a little more often, and the same photo shows on both your Home Screen widget and the app. Photos you’d rather not see can be tucked away with “Not right now” — nothing is deleted. No account, no ads.

iOS 18.0 or later / iPhone only, iPad not supported. The app interface is available in Japanese only.

Six tools for turning the page each day

  • Build a theme

    Name someone you love, a pet, or a memory, and pick one of six colors for it. Add photos from your device’s photo library, choosing several at once. You can create one theme.

  • Today’s photo

    Open the app and today’s photo fills the screen. It stays the same for the whole day, so the widget and the app always show the same photo. The theme name and date sit above it, the date it was taken below.

  • See again

    A button on today’s photo. It isn’t a favorite — pressing it just makes that photo more likely to come back around. Press it again to undo.

  • Not right now

    Set a photo aside for a week or a month when it doesn’t match how you feel. The photo is never deleted, and you can bring it back anytime from theme editing.

  • Home Screen widget

    Small and medium widgets show today’s photo, the theme name, and the date. Tap to open the app. Update timing can shift a little — that’s how iOS widgets work.

  • This month’s turn

    At the end of the month, up to four photos you marked “See again” that month are gathered into one. If there are fewer than four, photos you met that month fill the rest. Save it as a tall card image to your Photos app.

Photos aren’t picked at random, and not by popularity either. A photo you marked “See again” comes back a little more often, while ones shown recently or the day before are less likely to appear. You can still meet a photo you’d forgotten, and the ones that matter come back a little sooner.

Creating more than one theme, adding unlimited photos (the free plan holds up to 50), choosing a layout for the monthly card, and looking back through every past turn are planned for OmoiMekuri Pro, coming soon. See Support for details.

Your photos stay on your device

Your photos, theme name, capture dates, and photo identifiers are stored only on your device and are never sent anywhere. Photos are never copied into the app — only the photo library’s identifier is stored and read again each time. We run anonymous usage measurement to improve the app, but it only sends the type of action taken, never the content of your photos or theme name. See the Privacy Policy for the full picture.
