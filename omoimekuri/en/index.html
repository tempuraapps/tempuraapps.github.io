OmoiMekuri

The photo that matters,
comes to find you today.

Add photos to a theme, and one photo arrives each day — the same one on your Home Screen widget and in the app. Photos you’d rather not see can be tucked away with “Not right now” — nothing is deleted. Every day you have kept stays in a month calendar to look back on. No account, no ads.

iOS 18.0 or later / iPhone

Six tools for turning the page each day

  • Build a theme

    Name someone you love, a pet, or a place, then add photos from your device’s photo library, choosing several at once. Around eight or more keeps the same faces from cycling too quickly. One theme on the free plan, as many as you like with Pro.

  • Today’s photo

    Open the app and today’s photo fills the screen. It stays the same for the whole day, so the widget and the app always show the same photo. The theme name and date sit above it, the date it was taken below. Tap to see it full screen.

  • Turns

    Each day’s photo sits in its square on a month calendar. Tap one to see it large. The last three months on the free plan, every month with Pro.

  • Not right now

    Set a photo aside for a month when it doesn’t match how you feel. The photo is never deleted — bring it back with “Undo” right after, or with one tap in theme editing.

  • Home Screen widget

    Small fills with the photo and a date badge; medium sets the photo beside this month’s calendar. Tap to open the app on Today. Update timing can shift a little — that’s how iOS widgets work.

  • A month in one image

    The share button on Turns renders that month’s calendar as a tall card image. Send it as it is, or save it to your Photos app from the iOS share sheet.

Photos aren’t picked at random, and not by popularity either. Ones that haven’t appeared much are a little more likely, while ones shown in the past week — and yesterday’s — are less likely. You can still meet a photo you’d forgotten, without the same faces coming round again and again. There is no reordering and no picking by hand.

Building as many themes as you like, adding as many photos as you like, and looking back at every past turn come with OmoiMekuri Pro (US$1.99 per month or US$19.99 per year, auto-renewing). The free plan still gives you one theme, 50 photos, and the last three months of turns. Today’s photo, “Not right now,” the widget, the Turns calendar, sharing a month as a card, and backing up your data are all free. See Support for details.

Your photos stay on your device

Your photos, theme name, capture dates, and photo identifiers are stored only on your device and are never sent anywhere. Photos are never copied into the app — only the photo library’s identifier is stored and read again each time. We run anonymous usage measurement to improve the app, but it only sends the type of action taken, never the content of your photos or theme name. See the Privacy Policy for the full picture.
