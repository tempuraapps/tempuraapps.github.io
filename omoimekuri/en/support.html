Frequently asked questions

Where do I start?

The first time you open the app, it takes you straight to building a theme. Name someone you love, a pet, or a place, then choose photos from your photo library. That’s it — today’s photo appears on the Today tab. You can select several photos at once, and around eight or more keeps the same faces from cycling too quickly.

How is today’s photo chosen? The same photos keep showing up

Photos aren’t picked at random, and not by popularity either. Ones that haven’t appeared much are a little more likely, while ones shown in the past week are less likely. Yesterday’s photo is avoided too, unless it’s the only candidate left. With only a few photos in a theme, the same handful cycles through quickly — adding more, around eight or more, changes how it feels. Once chosen, the photo stays the same all day, and each theme is decided separately.

There’s a photo I’d rather not see

Choose “Not right now” below today’s photo and it stays out of the rotation for one month. There is no choice of duration. The photo is never deleted — bring it back with one tap in theme editing, or with “Undo” in the toast that appears right after.

Can I reorder photos or pick today’s photo myself?

No. One photo arriving on its own each day is the heart of the app. You can open any photo whenever you like from the Turns calendar or the grid in theme editing.

I’m not sure how to add the widget, or it doesn’t switch when the date changes

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap “Add Widget,” and choose OmoiMekuri in the small or medium size. Widget updates are handled by iOS, so it can take a little while to switch after the date changes. Opening the app always updates it to that moment’s photo.

A photo says “not found”

The photo may have been deleted in the Photos app, or your access permission may have changed. Choose photos again from theme editing.

Can I use it with “Selected Photos Only” access?

Yes. Photos you have not selected never appear in the daily rotation, and only photos within the access you’ve granted can be added to your theme.

What is the “Turns” tab?

A month calendar. The photo that arrived on each day sits in its square, and tapping one opens it large. Move between months with the arrows beside the month name. The free plan covers the last three months including this one; OmoiMekuri Pro opens up every month you have.

Can I turn a month into a single image?

Yes. Tap the share button at the top right of the Turns tab and that month’s calendar becomes a tall card image, then the iOS share sheet opens. To put it in your Photos app, choose “Save Image” there (this needs the add-only photo permission — check it in Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos). The button is disabled for months with no records.

I want to change the theme name or photos, or add another theme

Tap Edit at the top right of the Today tab to rename the theme and add or remove photos. To switch or add a theme, tap the theme name at the top left of the same screen. A theme can only be deleted from the bottom of the edit screen, behind a two-step confirmation.

Does my data sync to my other devices?

It does not. The theme name and photo identifiers are stored only on your device. You can export and import them as a JSON file from Settings > Back up your data (both are free). Note, however, that the file holds no photos — only markers pointing to where each photo sits in your photo library. Importing on a different device can leave photos showing as “not found,” so treat it as a way to restore onto the same device. Importing replaces your current data.

What happens to my data if I delete the app?

The theme and records stored on your device, such as “Not right now,” are removed with it. The original photos in your photo library are not affected. If you want to keep them, export from Settings > Back up your data first.

About OmoiMekuri Pro

What does Pro unlock?

Three things.

  • Build as many themes as you like — the free plan holds one
  • Add as many photos as you like — the free plan holds 50 per theme (theme editing shows how many you have left)
  • Look back at all your past turns — the free plan covers the last three months including this one

Everything else — today’s photo, “Not right now,” the Home Screen widget, the Turns calendar and sharing a month as a card, and backing up your data (both export and import) — is free. There is no limit on how many days you can keep.

What does it cost?

US$1.99 per month or US$19.99 per year (prices vary by country or region; the App Store shows your local price). There is no free trial. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase, and renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends. The renewal charge is taken within 24 hours of the end of the period.

I purchased but Pro isn’t showing

Try “Restore purchases” on the Pro screen. You need to be signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you purchased with. If it still doesn’t appear, please get in touch.

I want to cancel my subscription

Cancellation happens on the App Store side. Open Settings > your name > Subscriptions on your iPhone, choose OmoiMekuri, and select “Cancel Subscription.” The same screen opens from “Manage subscription” under Settings > Plan in the app.

After cancelling, Pro features stay available until the period you have paid for ends. Cancelling on its own does not produce a prorated refund (see “I’d like a refund” below).

I want to switch between monthly and yearly

Switch from the same Settings > your name > Subscriptions screen. The two OmoiMekuri plans sit at the same level, so a switch takes effect at your next renewal date — it does not swap immediately, and no prorated refund is involved. Until then, the plan you are paying for continues.

What happens to my themes and photos if I cancel?

Nothing is lost. Your themes, the photos you added, and every past turn stay exactly as they were. Only the three things above stop — whatever sits beyond the free limits simply becomes hidden, not deleted. Subscribe again and it all comes back as it was. We never delete your themes or photos on our side.

I want to check my renewal date

Open Settings > your name > Subscriptions to see the next renewal date and amount. Auto-renewal continues unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends.

I’d like a refund

Payments are handled by Apple, and refund decisions are Apple’s. Please apply at reportaproblem.apple.com. We are not able to process refunds ourselves.
