Frequently asked questions

Where do I start?

Open the Theme tab, name your theme, pick a color, and choose photos from your photo library. That’s it — “Today’s photo” will appear. You can select several photos at once.

What does “See again” do?

It’s a button on today’s photo. It isn’t a favorite — pressing it just makes that photo more likely to come back around. Press it again to undo.

The same photos keep showing up, or some never do

Photos aren’t picked at random, and not by popularity either. Ones shown recently or the day before are less likely to appear, and a photo you marked “See again” comes back a little more often. With only a few photos in a theme, the same handful cycles through quickly — adding more, around eight or more, changes how it feels.

There’s a photo I’d rather not see

Choose “Not right now” from today’s photo to set it aside for a week or a month. The photo is never deleted, and you can bring it back anytime from theme editing.

I’m not sure how to add the widget, or it doesn’t switch when the date changes

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap “Add Widget,” and choose OmoiMekuri in the small or medium size. Widget updates are handled by iOS, so it can take a little while to switch after the date changes. Opening the app always updates it to that moment’s photo.

A photo says “not found”

The photo may have been deleted in the Photos app, or your access permission may have changed. Choose photos again from theme editing.

Can I use it with “Selected Photos Only” access?

Yes. Photos you have not selected never appear in the daily rotation, and only photos within the access you’ve granted can be added to your theme.

What happens if “This month’s turn” has fewer than four photos?

If fewer than four photos were marked “See again” that month, photos you met that month fill the rest, up to four.

I can’t save the card from “This month’s turn”

Saving the card requires the add-only photo permission. Check OmoiMekuri’s permission in Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos.

I want to change the theme name or photos

Open the Theme tab and edit it — you can rename the theme and add or remove photos.

Does my data sync to my other devices?

It does not. The theme name and photo identifiers are stored only on your device. There is currently no export or import feature for backing them up.

What happens to my data if I delete the app?

The theme and records stored on your device, such as “See again” and “Not right now,” are removed with it. The original photos in your photo library are not affected.

About OmoiMekuri Pro

When will OmoiMekuri Pro be available?

It is currently in preparation and not yet available. When it launches, we plan to offer creating more than one theme, unlimited photos (the free plan holds up to 50), a choice of layouts for the monthly card, and looking back through every past turn. Pricing and other details will be announced on this page when it launches.

Today’s photo, “See again,” “Not right now,” the Home Screen widget, and viewing and saving “This month’s turn” — the core experience is free either way.

Requirements

iPhone running iOS 18.0 or later. iPad is not supported. The app interface is available in Japanese only.
