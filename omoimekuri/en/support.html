Frequently asked questions

Where do I start?

The first time you open the app, it takes you straight to building a collection. Name someone you love, a pet, or a place, then choose photos from your photo library. That’s it — today’s photo appears on the Home tab. You can select several photos at once, and the more you add, the longer before a face comes round again.

How is today’s photo chosen? The same photos keep showing up

Photos aren’t drawn by lottery. Each collection keeps one rotation and every day moves one step along it, so no photo comes round again until the whole collection has been through once, and the same photo never lands two days running. With only a few photos the rotation is short, so faces come round again sooner; the more you add, the longer the gap. Once chosen, the photo stays the same all day, and each collection is decided separately. Change the order under Settings > Flip order.

There’s a photo I’d rather not see

Touch and hold today’s photo and choose “Hide for now” — it stays out of the rotation for one month. There is no choice of duration. The photo is never deleted — bring it back with one tap in the Edit collection screen, or with “Undo” in the toast that appears right after.

I’d like to add a note to a photo

Tap a photo to open it full screen, then tap the pencil button at the top right to write a note. A note belongs to the photo, so it comes back with that photo the next time it arrives. Your note appears on the date line of the Home tab and when you open that day on the Calendar tab. It can also fill the medium Home Screen widget — choose between Calendar and Note under Settings > Widget > Medium widget. Notes are free, and nothing you write ever leaves your device.

Can I change the flip order or pick today’s photo myself?

Yes. Under Settings > Flip order you can choose Random, Date added, or Custom. With Custom, touch and hold a photo in the Edit collection screen to reorder it. To change today’s photo itself, swipe left or right on the Home tab to step back and forth, or open a photo full screen and tap “Start from this photo.” Touch and hold a day on the Calendar tab to swap that day’s photo. Whichever order you choose, no photo comes round again until the whole collection has been through once.

I’m not sure how to add the widget, or it doesn’t switch when the date changes

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap “Add Widget,” and choose OmoiMekuri in the small or medium size. Widget updates are handled by iOS, so it can take a little while to switch after the date changes. Opening the app always updates it to that moment’s photo.

A photo says “not found”

The photo may have been deleted in the Photos app, or your access permission may have changed. Choose photos again from the Edit collection screen.

Can I use it with “Selected Photos Only” access?

Yes. Photos you have not selected never appear in the daily rotation, and only photos within the access you’ve granted can be added to your collection.

What is the “Calendar” tab?

A month calendar. The photo that arrived on each day sits in its square, and tapping one opens it large. Move between months with the arrows beside the month name. The free plan covers the current month and the three months before it (four months in all); OmoiMekuri Pro opens up every month you have.

Can I turn a month into a single image?

Yes. Tap the share button at the top right of the Calendar tab and that month’s calendar becomes a tall card image, then the iOS share sheet opens. To put it in your Photos app, choose “Save Image” there (this needs the add-only photo permission — check it in Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos). The button is disabled for months with no records.

I want to change the collection name or photos, or add another collection

Tap Edit at the top right of the Home tab to rename the collection and add or remove photos. To switch or add a collection, use the Collection tab at the bottom, or tap the collection name at the top left of the Home tab. A collection can only be deleted from the bottom of the edit screen, behind a two-step confirmation.

Does my data sync to my other devices?

It does not. The collection name and photo identifiers are stored only on your device. You can export and import them as a JSON file from Settings > Data (both are free). Note, however, that the file holds no photos — only markers pointing to where each photo sits in your photo library. Importing on a different device can leave photos showing as “not found,” so treat it as a way to restore onto the same device. Importing replaces your current data.

What happens to my data if I delete the app?

The collection and records stored on your device, such as “Hide for now,” are removed with it. The original photos in your photo library are not affected. If you want to keep them, export from Settings > Data first.

About OmoiMekuri Pro

What does Pro unlock?

Four things.

  • Add as many photos as you like
  • Build as many collections as you like
  • Look back at all your past days
  • Choose any of the five photo frames

What does the free plan include?

The Edit collection screen shows how many photos you have left. Anything beyond the free limits becomes hidden, never deleted.

Everything else — today’s photo, “Hide for now,” the Home Screen widget, the Calendar tab and sharing a month as a card, and backing up your data (both export and import) — is free. There is no limit on how many days you can keep.

What does it cost?

US$1.99 per month or US$19.99 per year (prices vary by country or region; the App Store shows your local price). There is no free trial. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase, and renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends. The renewal charge is taken within 24 hours of the end of the period.

I purchased but Pro isn’t showing

Try “Restore purchases” on the Pro screen. You need to be signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you purchased with. If it still doesn’t appear, please get in touch.

I want to cancel my subscription

Cancellation happens on the App Store side. Open Settings > your name > Subscriptions on your iPhone, choose OmoiMekuri, and select “Cancel Subscription.” The same screen opens from “Manage subscription” under Settings > Plan in the app.

After cancelling, Pro features stay available until the period you have paid for ends. Cancelling on its own does not produce a prorated refund (see “I’d like a refund” below).

I want to switch between monthly and yearly

Switch from the same Settings > your name > Subscriptions screen. The two OmoiMekuri plans sit at the same level, so a switch takes effect at your next renewal date — it does not swap immediately, and no prorated refund is involved. Until then, the plan you are paying for continues.

What happens to my collections and photos if I cancel?

Nothing is lost. Your collections, the photos you added, and every past day stay exactly as they were. Only the four things above stop — whatever sits beyond the free limits simply becomes hidden, not deleted. Subscribe again and it all comes back as it was. We never delete your collections or photos on our side.

I want to check my renewal date

Open Settings > your name > Subscriptions to see the next renewal date and amount. Auto-renewal continues unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends.

I’d like a refund

Payments are handled by Apple, and refund decisions are Apple’s. Please apply at reportaproblem.apple.com. We are not able to process refunds ourselves.

Requirements

iPhone running iOS 18.0 or later. iPad is not supported. The app is available in Japanese and English.
