Frequently asked questions

How do I number my physical reeds?

Write it in pencil on the back of the reed, or on the divider of your reed case. Use the same number the App assigns automatically when you log the box (#1, #2, and so on), and the record on screen always stays linked to the one in your hand.

Can I change the break-in days or minutes?

Yes, under Settings > Customize > Break-in Plan. A change applies immediately to boxes already mid-break-in, not just new ones.

How does the App count "day N" of a break-in plan?

It counts days you actually logged a session, not calendar days. A day you skip doesn't move the count forward. You don't have to play every single day — the plan follows your own pace.

I played longer than the suggested time

You'll see a confirmation if you're about to go over, but you can still save the session. The break-in plan's minutes are a guideline, not a hard limit. Use your own judgment about how the reed is holding up.

The same reed keeps getting recommended (or never does)

"Today's reed" is chosen from rest days and recent ratings. To keep a specific reed out of the running, set it to resting, retired, or excluded. Conversely, a reed marked as your performance candidate is not prioritized in everyday "today's reed" picks — that's deliberate, so it doesn't wear out before the performance.

I logged a reed by mistake, or want to remove some

A reed with no session history can be deleted with a swipe from the box's detail screen. A reed that already has history should be retired instead — the records stay, and it drops out of today's picks.

Notifications aren't arriving

Check that the App is allowed to send notifications in your iPhone's Settings app. Notifications only arrive on days when a reed is in the middle of its break-in plan — if none are, nothing is sent that day.

I'm switching to a new phone

Use Settings > Data and "Export JSON" to save a file (JSON format), then "Import JSON" on the new device. Exporting and importing are Reed Biyori Pro features.

Does my data sync to my other devices?

It does not. Your boxes, reeds, and session records are stored only on your device.

Is anything I log sent elsewhere?

The content you log is never sent. A box's brand, a reed's number, ratings, memos, and session times stay on your device. The only external communication is purchase processing for the paid plan and anonymous usage measurement (app launches and updates, the category of a logging action, opening the Pro screen, and the result of a purchase or restore). See the Privacy Policy for details.

What happens to my data if I delete the app?

The records stored on your device are removed with it. Before deleting, save anything you need using Settings > Data and "Export JSON" (Reed Biyori Pro).

About Reed Biyori Pro

What does Pro unlock?

Three things:

  • Unlimited boxes and reeds — free is up to one box
  • Dark mode and automatic switching — free is light appearance only
  • Export & import JSON — for moving to a new phone

Everything you need for day-to-day use is free — logging one box's worth of reeds, the break-in guide, today's recommended reed, notifications, and session history. Pro adds only the three things above.

What does it cost?

US$1.99 per month or US$19.99 per year (prices vary by country or region; the App Store shows your local price). There is no free trial. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase, and renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends. The renewal charge is taken within 24 hours of the end of the period.

I purchased Pro but it is not showing

Try "Restore Purchases" in the app. You need to be signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you used to buy. If it still does not appear, please get in touch.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Cancelling is done on the App Store side. Open Settings > your name > Subscriptions, choose Reed Biyori, and select "Cancel Subscription". You can reach the same screen from "Manage subscription" under Settings > Plan in the app.

After cancelling you keep the Pro features until the period you have paid for ends. Cancelling by itself does not produce a pro-rated refund (if you want a refund, see "I would like a refund" below).

If I cancel, what happens to my records and settings?

Your records aren't deleted, and files you've already exported stay with you. What stops working are the three things above. The settings you chose are still saved — go back to Pro and your previous state returns.

How do I check my renewal date?

The same Settings > your name > Subscriptions screen shows your next renewal date and price. Auto-renewal continues unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends.

I would like a refund

Payments are handled by Apple, and refund decisions are Apple's. Please request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot process refunds ourselves.
