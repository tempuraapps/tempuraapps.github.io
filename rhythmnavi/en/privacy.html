RhythmNavi Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

About the app · Support · 日本語

RhythmNavi ("the App") is provided by TempuraApps ("we"). This policy explains how the App handles user information.

1. Information we collect

The App does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. Photos of sheet music you take, the rhythms you enter, your practice records, and the names you give your practice sets are stored only on your device and are never transmitted to or held on servers operated by us or by third parties. The only exception is the usage measurement described in section 3, which sends a limited set of information to an external service. It never includes photos, audio, the contents of a rhythm, or the names you give your practice sets.

2. Accounts and network use

No account registration is required to use the App. Photos, rhythms, and practice records stay on your device and are not transmitted externally. The App does, however, communicate with a limited external service for the usage measurement described below (section 3). See section 4 for how purchase infrastructure is handled.

3. Usage analytics (PostHog)

To improve quality and features, the App measures anonymous usage with the analytics service PostHog. What is collected is the fact that you performed one of the actions below, together with any count, tempo value, or percentage involved, and the kind of option chosen:

  • App launches, updates, and moving to and from the background
  • Opening the RhythmNavi Pro screen (which screen you came from)
  • Starting, completing, or failing a purchase (plan type, whether the build has billing enabled, and the reason for a failure), and restoring a purchase (no plan type is sent for a restore)
  • Creating a practice set (how it was created, the number of notes, and the number of measures)
  • Finishing a sheet-music scan (whether it succeeded, and how many notes were read)
  • Previewing a rhythm in the editor (tempo and number of notes)
  • Starting a practice run (judging method, tempo, repeat setting, guide-sound setting, and whether it is a sample)
  • Finishing a practice run and viewing the result (judging method, tempo, percentage of "on time" notes, number of missed notes, number of extra notes, and the kind of tendency detected)
  • Accepting a tempo suggestion (whether it raised the tempo, lowered it, or repeated at the same speed)
  • Adding or removing the sample data
  • Exporting or importing data (the number of practice sets and records)

Nothing beyond this scope is sent. The photo of your sheet music, any part of it, the audio picked up by the microphone, the notes read from a score, the rhythms you enter, and the names you give your practice sets are never transmitted. Only numbers, booleans, and values chosen from a fixed set can be sent; there is simply no mechanism for text or images you provide to be attached. The rage-click feature, which detects rapid tapping and sends the on-screen text, is disabled, because your own content could be caught up in it.

Alongside this, technical information that PostHog adds automatically may be included (app version, OS name and version, device model, and the app's display language). The time zone is stripped before sending, because it can imply an approximate region. IP addresses are configured to be discarded on the analytics side, and geographic and location inference is disabled. Detailed location such as city or latitude and longitude is never collected under any circumstances. None of this is intended to identify a specific individual, and none of it is tied to an account. Data is stored in the EU region.

Each record sent carries an anonymous identifier generated per installation. It exists so that actions from the same installation can be viewed together; it is not linked to a name, email address, account, or device identifier, and it is not used to identify you. Deleting and reinstalling the App produces a new identifier.

We have no means of connecting that identifier back to you. As a result, we cannot fulfil requests to extract or individually delete data for a particular user. If you want to stop the measurement entirely, delete the App; nothing is sent after that.

The App shows no advertising and performs no ad tracking (IDFA or otherwise). We do not use the collected information for advertising purposes, and we do not provide or sell it to third parties.

4. Purchases (RevenueCat)

Purchases and restores of RhythmNavi Pro go through Apple's App Store, and the purchase and subscription state is managed with the billing service RevenueCat. Payment details such as credit card numbers are handled by Apple and are never received by us. Purchases are managed under an anonymous identifier and are not linked to personal information such as your name or email address. Note that RevenueCat is a service operated by a company in the United States.

5. Camera and photos (on-device only, never transmitted)

The App uses the camera so you can photograph the measures you want to practise. You can also pick an existing photo from your library instead. Images you capture or select are stored only on your device and are never transmitted to servers operated by us or by third parties.

Reading notes from a score also happens entirely on your device. The image is never sent to an external server for analysis, and scanning works with no internet connection at all.

Selecting from your photo library uses a mechanism that hands the App only the single image you chose (PHPicker); the App never gains access to your library as a whole.

If you decline camera access, you can still use the App fully by choosing a photo from your library, or by entering a rhythm by hand without a photo.

6. Microphone (no recording, never transmitted)

The App can listen to your playing and judge your timing. The microphone is used only while that feature is active.

Audio is analysed on the spot, on your device, and only the moment each note begins is extracted for judging. The audio itself is never saved to a file, and it is never transmitted to servers operated by us or by third parties. Once analysed, the audio is discarded from memory.

If you decline microphone access, every practice in the App remains fully usable by tapping the screen to keep time instead.

7. Practice records (on-device only, never transmitted)

The names of your practice sets, their settings, the rhythms you enter, and your practice records are stored in a single file on your device and are never transmitted to servers operated by us or by third parties. Photos you capture are stored in an area of the device reserved for the App. The App does not use location, health, or contacts data, and never asks for access to them. There is no syncing between devices; your records stay on the device you use.

8. Backing up your data

The App can export your practice sets, rhythms, and records to a file (in JSON format), and import one back. You will find both under "Data" in Settings, and both are available free of charge.

An exported file contains what you created — practice set names, the rhythms you entered, and your practice records — as-is (photos are not included). The file is placed wherever you choose to save it and is thereafter under your control. We do not access it, and it is not transmitted anywhere. An import reads only the file you select, on your device, and replaces the data on your device with its contents. Nothing is transmitted to servers operated by us or by third parties. Before an import, your existing data is set aside on the device first.

9. Provision to third parties

We do not provide or sell your information to third parties for advertising purposes. Information sent to the external service described in section 3 (PostHog) is processed under that service's own privacy policy. The same applies to RevenueCat as described in section 4.

10. Deleting your data

Data stored on your device can be erased with "Delete all data" in Settings, or by deleting the App itself. Photos you captured are deleted at the same time.

11. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy as features are added or changed. Any significant change will be announced on this page.
