Frequently asked questions

Where do I start?

A short introduction appears the first time you open the App. After that, tap the + button at the bottom right of the home screen to create a practice, and follow the screen from there. If you would rather try things out with something already in place, choose "Add sample data" under "Data" in Settings.

How do I create a practice?

Tapping + gives you two options: scan it from sheet music or enter it by hand. If you have a photo of the music, try scanning first and switch to entering by hand if it does not work out. You can also edit anything the scan produced.

Scanning my sheet music does not work

Scanning works best on a photo where one or two measures fill the frame. Please check the following:

  • Capture a single system. A photo of a whole page leaves each note only a few pixels across, which cannot be read. If you see "more than one system is in the photo", narrow your selection to one or two measures.
  • Get closer. "Too small" means the staff lines are too close together in the image. Move in and take it again.
  • Keep it straight and flat. A heavily rotated photo, or one where the page curves into the spine of a book, cannot be read.
  • Keep the lighting even. Half-shadowed or blown-out photos are difficult.
  • Choose a single melodic line. Scanning stops if chords (notes stacked vertically) or repeat signs are present.

Scanning runs entirely on your device, so it works with no signal at all.

Why does it ask me questions while scanning?

It asks only about the places it could not settle from the image alone — questions like "which symbol is this?" or "is this a triplet?". If you are not sure, choose "I don't know". Scanning then stops and switches you to entering by hand: guessing at a rhythm and letting you practise the wrong thing would be far worse. The same switch happens if the number of questions grows too large.

The scan got a note wrong

A confirmation screen always follows a scan. Tap the note there and correct it. Scanning is not perfect, so we recommend looking it over once before you start practising.

Which note values are supported?

Whole, half, quarter, eighth, sixteenth, and thirty-second notes, along with their rests. Dots (on everything except whole and thirty-second notes), ties, and triplets are also supported. Time signatures can be 4/4, 3/4, 2/4, or 6/8.

Do the notes show the right pitches?

No. Notes move up and down on screen only to show that the pitch changes here; they do not represent the actual pitches in your music. RhythmNavi is about rhythm, so it holds no pitch data at all.

Can I change the tempo?

Yes. Both in practice and in the editor, the + and − buttons on the tempo row move it one step at a time, and holding them down moves faster. Tapping the number lets you type a tempo directly, and tapping TAP a few times sets the tempo from your tapping. The range is 40–120 BPM (40–80 for 6/8). A practice set containing very fast notes stops at the highest tempo it can play cleanly, and the screen tells you why.

What is the difference between tap and microphone judging?

With tap judging you tap the screen in time. With microphone judging (Pro), the App listens to what you actually play, so your hands stay on the instrument. The accuracy, the advice, and the tempo suggestions are identical either way. When the microphone is in use, the audio is analysed on the spot and never saved or transmitted.

I would rather not grant microphone access

That is fine. Tap judging takes you through every practice from start to finish. The same is true of the camera: without it you can pick a photo from your library, or skip the photo and enter a rhythm by hand.

What do "early", "on time", and "late" mean?

For each note, the App shows how far you were from the reference position, in milliseconds. On time is teal, early is a blue left-pointing arrow, and late is a red right-pointing one. The number and the direction are shown alongside the colour so the result is readable even if the colours are hard to tell apart.

How is the next tempo decided?

A suggestion for your next tempo appears under the result: a little faster after a good run, a little slower after a shaky one. Tapping it starts the same rhythm again at that speed.

What are the syllables under the notes?

They are the Japanese rhythm syllables ("ta", "ta-a", "un") used in school music lessons: ta where you play, a where you hold, and u where you rest. Saying them out loud helps the rhythm settle before your fingers get involved.

Where are the photos I take stored?

Only in an area of your device reserved for RhythmNavi. They are not saved to your photo library and are never transmitted anywhere. Deleting a practice set deletes its photo along with it.

Is my data synced across devices?

No. Practice sets, rhythms, and records are stored only on the device you use. Under "Data" in Settings you can export and import them as a JSON file, which is how to move them to another device or keep a backup (both are free). An exported file does not include the photos — opened on another device, the measures appear without an image. Importing replaces your current data, and your existing data is set aside automatically first.

What happens to my data if I delete the App?

Practice sets, rhythms, records, and photos stored on the device are erased. If you need them, export a copy from "Data" in Settings before deleting.

I want to delete everything

"Delete all data" in Settings erases everything on the device at once. It cannot be undone, so we recommend exporting a copy first if you might want it later.

Can I try it with sample data?

Choose "Add sample data" under "Data" in Settings and several practice sets appear, so you can try the whole flow. "Remove sample data" in the same place clears them again at any time. Samples do not count towards the free limit of three saved practice sets.

RhythmNavi Pro

What does Pro add?

Four things:

  • Microphone judging — practise with your hands on the instrument
  • Save as many practice sets as you like
  • Look back through your full history
  • Weak-spot analysis — see where you tend to stumble

Reaching the limit never removes or hides a practice set you have already saved. It only stops you saving new ones. You can still build a fourth one, practise it, and see your result. Records older than seven days also remain on your device and become visible again the moment you go Pro.

Everything else — scanning sheet music, the editor, changing the tempo, tap judging, per-run results and advice, tempo suggestions, JSON export and import, the appearance setting, and deleting all data — is free.

How much does it cost?

¥300 per month or ¥3,000 per year (prices in Japan; they differ by country and region). The yearly plan costs exactly ten months of the monthly one. There is no free trial. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase, and renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends. The renewal charge is applied within 24 hours of the end of the period.

I purchased Pro but it is not active

Try "Restore purchases" on the Pro screen. You need to be signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you used to buy it. If it still does not appear, please contact us at the address above.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Cancellation happens on Apple's side. On your iPhone, open Settings > your name > Subscriptions, select RhythmNavi, and choose "Cancel Subscription". "Manage subscription" under Settings > Plan in the App opens the same screen.

After cancelling, Pro features remain available until the period you have paid for ends. Cancellation itself does not produce a pro-rated refund (see "I would like a refund" below).

How do I switch between monthly and yearly?

From the same Settings > your name > Subscriptions screen. RhythmNavi's two plans sit at the same level, so a switch takes effect at your next renewal date — it does not change immediately, and no pro-rated refund is issued. Until then, the plan you are paying for continues.

What happens to my practice sets and records if I cancel?

Nothing is deleted. The four features above simply stop: you cannot save a fourth practice set, judging returns to tapping, and records older than seven days and the weak-spot analysis are no longer visible (the records themselves remain, and come back the moment you go Pro again).

How do I check my renewal date?

The same Settings > your name > Subscriptions screen shows your next renewal date and amount. Automatic renewal continues unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends.

I would like a refund

Payment is handled by Apple, and refund decisions are Apple's to make. Please apply at reportaproblem.apple.com. We are unable to process refunds ourselves.
