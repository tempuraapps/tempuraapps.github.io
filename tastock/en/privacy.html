TASTOCK Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

About the app · Support · 日本語

TASTOCK ("the App") is provided by TempuraApps ("we"). This policy explains how the App handles user information.

1. Information we collect

The App does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. Everything you record or enter — for each seasoning: its name, category, origin, where and when you bought it, the date you opened it, its best-before date, flavor notes, flavor intensity, a memo, and whether it’s a Favorite; for each recipe: the dish name, ingredients, steps, cook time, and rating; and any photos you choose — is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to or held on servers operated by us or by third parties. The only exceptions are the usage measurement described in section 3 and the Pro purchase processing described in section 4, each of which sends a limited set of information to external services. Neither includes your name, the content you record or enter, or your photos.

2. Accounts and network use

No account registration is required to use the App. The data you record or enter, and your photos, stay on your device and are not transmitted externally. The App does, however, communicate with a limited set of external services for the usage measurement described below (section 3) and for purchase processing (section 4).

3. Usage analytics (PostHog)

To improve quality and features, the App measures anonymous usage with the analytics service PostHog. What is collected is limited to (a) app launches, updates, and moving to and from the background, (b) finishing the first-run guide (including whether you read it through or skipped it), and (c) opening the paid plan screen (including which feature it was opened from), starting a purchase of a paid plan, that purchase going through, that purchase not going through (the monthly or annual category, and the category of reason it failed), and restoring a purchase. Adding, viewing, or searching seasonings and recipes is not measured at all. We do not send personal information such as your name or email address, nor the content you record or enter (seasoning names, notes, recipes, or photos). Rage-click detection, which would send on-screen text, is disabled because the content you log could be caught up in it.

Technical information automatically attached by PostHog may also be included: the App version, your OS type and version, your device type, and the App's display language. Your time zone is stripped before sending, because it can be used to infer your approximate region. IP addresses are configured to be discarded on the analytics side, and we have specified that no region or location be inferred from them. Finer locations such as your city or your coordinates are never collected. None of this is intended to identify a specific individual, and none of it is linked to an account. The data is stored in the EU region.

Because the information sent is not tied to a specific individual, it cannot be retrieved or deleted per user. If you wish to stop the measurement entirely, please delete the App; nothing is sent after it is removed.

The App does not serve advertising or perform advertising tracking (IDFA and similar) of any kind. Nor do we use the information collected for advertising purposes, or provide or sell it to third parties.

4. Purchases (RevenueCat)

Purchases and restores of the App's paid features (TASTOCK Pro) are handled through Apple's App Store, and we use the subscription infrastructure service RevenueCat to manage purchases and entitlement status. Information such as your purchase status is therefore processed by RevenueCat. Payment details such as credit card numbers are handled by Apple and are never received by us. Purchases are managed under an anonymous identifier and are not linked to personal information such as your name or email address. Please note that RevenueCat is a service provided by a company based in the United States.

5. Photos

The App lets you attach photos of your seasonings and dishes to your records. A photo is only read when you choose it, and it is stored solely on your device, in the App's own storage. It is never sent to servers operated by us or by third parties, never used for advertising or marketing purposes, and never provided or sold to third parties. It is also excluded from the usage measurement in section 3.

Photo selection uses iOS's standard photo picker, so the App never gets access to your entire photo library. A photo is resized to 2000 pixels on its long edge or smaller before it is saved. Photos are removed when you delete the record they were attached to, or when you delete the App itself.

6. Notifications

Turning on notifications in Settings lets the App notify you with a local notification on your device when a seasoning's best-before date is approaching. The notification content is assembled entirely on your device and never passes through an external server. The App does not use push notifications (remote notifications) and never obtains a device token for that purpose. Notifications are off by default.

7. Data backup

The App can export your recorded data as a file (in JSON format) — TASTOCK Pro. An exported file is placed in the location you choose and is under your control. We do not access it. Note that exported files do not include photos.

8. Provision to third parties

We do not provide or sell your information to third parties for advertising purposes. Information sent to the external services described in sections 3 and 4 (PostHog and RevenueCat) for measurement and purchase processing is handled under each service's own privacy policy.

9. Deleting your data

Data stored on your device can be erased using the App's delete and reset features (Settings > Reset > Delete All Data), or by deleting the App itself.

10. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy as features are added or changed. If a change is significant, we will announce it on this page.
