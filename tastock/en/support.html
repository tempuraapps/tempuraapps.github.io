Frequently asked questions

Where do I start?

Try logging one seasoning you already have. Tap the + in the top right of Home or Shelf, add a photo and a name, and save. The category and opened status are fine left at their defaults.

Can I try it before adding my own seasonings?

Go to Settings > Data > Add sample data to fill in 12 sample seasonings and 6 sample recipes and look around every screen. You can remove the sample data from the same place, and anything you've logged yourself stays.

I don't know the best-before date

It's fine to leave it blank. It just shows as "No date" — it won't cause an error. You can add it later by editing the entry.

I bought another bottle of the same seasoning

Just add it as a new entry. You can keep it under the same name as a separate record. Since the purchase date and best-before date differ, keeping them as separate entries makes it easier to track expiration.

Is "not set" different from 0 for flavor intensity?

Yes. "not set" means "not measured yet," and 0 means "known to be none (not spicy)." If you're not sure, leave it as not set. Fields left as not set don't show up on the detail screen.

If I delete a seasoning, does the recipe go too?

No. The recipe stays — only its link to that seasoning is removed. The delete confirmation screen shows how many recipes are linked before you confirm.

Notifications aren't arriving

Check that "Best-before reminders" is on under Settings > Notifications, and that TASTOCK is allowed in your iPhone's notification settings. Notifications are off by default. If several seasonings are due for a reminder on the same day, they're combined into a single notification.

Can I buy the seasonings shown in Discover?

No, they're not for sale. They're there to introduce flavors you might not know yet — with flavor notes, what they go with, and tips plus a recipe for using them the first time. Once you have a bottle, tap "Add to my shelf" to log it with the name and flavor details carried over.

Where are photos stored?

Only on your device, in TASTOCK's own storage. They are never sent to our servers. Photos are resized to 2000 pixels on their long edge or smaller before they're saved. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Does my data sync to my other devices?

It does not. Seasonings, recipes, and photos are all stored only on your device. To move them when changing phones, use Settings > Data and "Export JSON" to save a file, then "Import JSON" on the new device. Exporting and importing are TASTOCK Pro features. Note that exported files do not include photos.

Is anything I log sent elsewhere?

The content you log is never sent. Seasoning names, notes, recipes, and photos stay on your device. The only external communication is purchase processing for the paid plan and anonymous usage measurement (app launches, finishing the first-run guide, opening the paid plan screen, and the result of a purchase or restore). See the Privacy Policy for details.

What happens to my data if I delete the app?

The records and photos stored on your device are removed with it. Before deleting, save anything you need using Settings > Data and "Export JSON" (TASTOCK Pro).

About TASTOCK Pro

What does Pro unlock?

Five things:

  • See every seasoning in Discover — the free version shows the first 3
  • Analyze your flavor trends — flavors you favor, categories you own, seasonings that appear most, and ones you haven't recorded yet
  • Choose from five color themes — Olive, Chili, Yuzu, Indigo, Charcoal
  • Set the appearance yourself — Automatic, Light, Dark
  • Export and import your records — JSON format

Everything else — adding, editing, and deleting seasonings and recipes, photos, best-before dates and notifications, search, filtering, and sorting, favorites, the first 3 seasonings in Discover, and the sample data — is free. There is no limit on how many seasonings, recipes, or photos you can log.

Can I use Discover on the free plan?

Yes. The first 3 are free to view. From the 4th onward they appear blurred, and what's under the blur is a real seasoning we're introducing too.

I purchased Pro but it is not showing

Try "Restore purchases" in the app. You need to be signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you used to buy. If it still does not appear, please get in touch.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Cancelling is done on the App Store side. Open Settings > your name > Subscriptions, choose TASTOCK, and select "Cancel Subscription". You can reach the same screen from "Manage subscription" under Settings > Plan in the app.

After cancelling you keep the Pro features until the period you have paid for ends. Cancelling by itself does not produce a pro-rated refund (if you want a refund, see "I would like a refund" below).

If I cancel, what happens to my records and settings?

Your records aren't deleted, and files you've already exported stay with you. What stops working are the five things above. The appearance returns to Light and the color theme to Olive, but the settings you chose are still saved — go back to Pro and your look returns.

How do I check my renewal date?

The same Settings > your name > Subscriptions screen shows your next renewal date and price. Auto-renewal continues unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends.

I would like a refund

Payments are handled by Apple, and refund decisions are Apple's. Please request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot process refunds ourselves.
